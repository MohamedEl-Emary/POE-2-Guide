## Overview

The Druid, added in patch 0.4, was the first class to combine Strength and Intelligence. It fights as a human casting nature magic, then shapeshifts into animal forms to brawl. Talismans are the Druid's signature weapon and enable the shapeshifting skills.

## Shapeshifting forms

- **Bear:** a tanky form that builds Rage and uses it on heavy slams.
- **Wolf:** a balanced form with fast cold-damage pounces that can call wolf companions.
- **Wyvern:** a flying form that breathes fire.

Forms count as separate weapon setups for <GlossaryTerm id="spirit" />, so you can plan around switching between them.

## Strengths and weaknesses

**Strengths:** very sturdy, flexible between caster and melee, and lots of build variety.

**Weaknesses:** switching forms and managing Rage adds complexity, and the class has only two Ascendancies so far.

## Which Ascendancy for a first character?

The **Shaman** strengthens the animal forms and Rage and is the more direct option. The **Oracle** is built around visions and fate, and even unlocks passive tree options other classes can't take. Oracle plant builds were among the cheapest strong starters in 0.5.5.

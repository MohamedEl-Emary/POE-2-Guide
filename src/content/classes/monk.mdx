## Overview

The Monk fights with a quarterstaff and is the fastest melee class in the game. Skills combine dashes, elemental strikes and quick retreats, so you're always moving.

Starting between Dexterity and Intelligence on the <GlossaryTerm id="passive-tree" />, the Monk mixes <GlossaryTerm id="evasion" /> and <GlossaryTerm id="energy-shield" /> for defence.

## How it plays

You move in, land a combination of strikes, and get out before enemies retaliate. Many Monk skills build up power that a later skill spends, which rewards learning a small rotation.

## Strengths and weaknesses

**Strengths:** very fast and fun, great mobility, and strong elemental damage.

**Weaknesses:** less forgiving than ranged or minion classes, and some skills need a rotation to reach full power.

<Callout type="beginner">
  Monk is a great second character once you know the basics. For a first character, expect a few more deaths while you learn when to retreat.
</Callout>

## Which Ascendancy for a first character?

The **Martial Artist**, added in 0.5, is popular for strong melee builds that are comfortable to play. The **Invoker** adds elemental power and defence from your gear. The **Acolyte of Chayula** is the most unusual, trading normal resources for darkness.

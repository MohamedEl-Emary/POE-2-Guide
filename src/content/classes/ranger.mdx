## Overview

The Ranger uses bows and arrows with lightning, cold, poison and physical skills. Her toolkit is built around shooting while moving and jumping away from danger, so she rarely has to stand still.

She starts in the Dexterity area of the <GlossaryTerm id="passive-tree" />, with lots of attack speed, projectile damage and <GlossaryTerm id="evasion" />.

## How it plays

You keep distance, pepper enemies with arrows and reposition constantly. Ranged attacks mean you can clear packs before they reach you and dodge boss attacks from afar.

## Strengths and weaknesses

**Strengths:** safe ranged play, fast movement, and very strong clear speed with the right skills.

**Weaknesses:** evasion-based defence can let big hits through, and you need to invest in accuracy and projectile scaling.

<Callout type="tip">
  Bow skills scale heavily with your bow. Upgrading your weapon is usually the biggest damage boost a Ranger can get.
</Callout>

## Which Ascendancy for a first character?

The **Deadeye** is the beginner pick: extra projectiles, more speed and long-range power. The **Pathfinder** focuses on flasks, poison and movement, and suits players who enjoy managing resources.

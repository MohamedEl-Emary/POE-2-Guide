## Overview

The Witch raises skeletons and other <GlossaryTerm id="minion">minions</GlossaryTerm>, curses enemies to weaken them, and casts chaos and fire spells. Many players' first PoE2 character is a Witch, because minions take a lot of the pressure off you.

## How it plays

A minion Witch hangs back while her army fights, adding curses and support spells. A caster Witch plays more like the Sorceress, but leans on chaos damage, damage over time and explosions.

Keeping minions alive uses <GlossaryTerm id="spirit" />, a resource you reserve rather than spend.

## Strengths and weaknesses

**Strengths:** minions are very safe and forgiving, the class has many playable styles, and curses help any build.

**Weaknesses:** minion builds can feel slow until your army is strong, and you depend on your minions' gear and gem levels.

<Callout type="beginner">
  If you're not sure what to play, a minion Witch is one of the safest choices for learning the game.
</Callout>

## Which Ascendancy for a first character?

The **Infernalist** is flexible and comes with a loyal hellhound. The **Lich** focuses on chaos damage, energy shield and curses. The **Blood Mage** spends life instead of mana, which is powerful but punishing if you're careless.

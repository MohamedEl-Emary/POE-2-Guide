## Overview

The Warrior swings two-handed or one-handed maces, smashes the ground with slams and shouts war cries that boost the next big hit. It is the most straightforward "walk up and hit it" class in the game.

Because it starts in the Strength corner of the <GlossaryTerm id="passive-tree" />, the Warrior has easy access to life, <GlossaryTerm id="armour" /> and stun power.

## How it plays

Most Warrior skills are slow but hit very hard. You learn to time attacks, dodge roll away from dangerous boss moves, then step back in. Many enemies get <GlossaryTerm id="stun">stunned</GlossaryTerm> or have their armour broken, which makes later hits land even harder.

## Strengths and weaknesses

**Strengths:** very tanky, simple to understand, huge single hits that feel great against bosses.

**Weaknesses:** slow attacks make clearing large packs take longer, and you have to stand close to dangerous enemies.

<Callout type="beginner">
  Pick the Warrior if you like a sturdy character and don't mind a slower pace. It's forgiving while you learn boss patterns.
</Callout>

## Which Ascendancy for a first character?

The **Titan** is the simplest: it makes your big hits bigger and your body tougher. The **Warbringer** adds totems and war cry synergy. The **Smith of Kitava** is the most unusual, adding fire to your weapon and letting you customise your chest armour.

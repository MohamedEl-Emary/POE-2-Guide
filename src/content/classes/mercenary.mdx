## Overview

The Mercenary is a crossbow specialist. Crossbow skills load different kinds of ammunition, and grenades can be thrown to set up combos, like covering enemies in gas and then igniting it.

Starting between Strength and Dexterity on the <GlossaryTerm id="passive-tree" />, the Mercenary has access to both <GlossaryTerm id="armour" /> and <GlossaryTerm id="evasion" />.

## How it plays

You fight from range like a Ranger but with more weight behind each shot. Reloading and choosing the right ammunition for the situation is part of the rhythm.

## Strengths and weaknesses

**Strengths:** strong ranged damage, sturdier than other ranged classes, and fun combo play with grenades.

**Weaknesses:** reloading can feel clunky at first, and some skills need setup before they shine.

<Callout type="tip">
  Grenades bounce and have a short fuse. Throw them slightly ahead of moving enemies.
</Callout>

## Which Ascendancy for a first character?

The **Gemling Legionnaire** is very flexible and pairs well with many skills, including grenades. The **Witchhunter** excels against spellcasters and boosts defences. The **Tactician** uses banners, pins and support fire, and also suits minion and totem builds.

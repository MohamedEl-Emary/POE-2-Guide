## Overview

The Huntress, added in patch 0.2, fights with a spear. Her skills let her stab up close, throw the spear at range, then leap back in. She often carries a buckler, a small shield that lets her parry.

She starts in the Dexterity area of the <GlossaryTerm id="passive-tree" />, sharing much of the Ranger's speed and <GlossaryTerm id="evasion" />.

## How it plays

The Huntress constantly moves between melee and medium range. Good players weave throws, leaps and parries together, which makes her feel agile but asks you to learn her toolkit.

## Strengths and weaknesses

**Strengths:** very mobile, flexible between ranged and melee, and has some of the strongest mapping builds in 0.5.5.

**Weaknesses:** her skill combos take time to learn, and she's less tanky than the Warrior or Mercenary.

## Which Ascendancy for a first character?

The **Spirit Walker**, added in 0.5, brings companion beasts and spirit-powered skills and is currently very popular. The **Amazon** focuses on precision, sustain and elemental power. The **Ritualist** sacrifices monsters to steal their powers and can wear an extra ring.

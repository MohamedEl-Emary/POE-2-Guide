## Overview

The Sorceress is the classic elemental mage. She fights with spells rather than weapon attacks, and her skills are designed to combine: freeze a group with cold, then shatter it, or soak enemies in lightning before a big fire blast.

She starts in the Intelligence area of the <GlossaryTerm id="passive-tree" />, which is full of spell damage, mana and <GlossaryTerm id="energy-shield" />.

## How it plays

You stay at range, set up crowd control and let spells do the work. Positioning matters more than for melee classes, because a Sorceress has less life and armour to absorb mistakes.

## Strengths and weaknesses

**Strengths:** excellent screen-clearing spells, lots of crowd control, and many viable elements to experiment with.

**Weaknesses:** fragile early on if you neglect defences, and mana management takes a little practice.

<Callout type="tip">
  Keep a spell that freezes or slows enemies on your bar while leveling. Enemies that can't reach you can't hurt you.
</Callout>

## Which Ascendancy for a first character?

The **Stormweaver** is the most direct, boosting elemental spells and storms. The **Disciple of Varashta** summons djinn allies and plays more like a durable minion user. The **Chronomancer** manipulates time and cooldowns, which is powerful but more technical.

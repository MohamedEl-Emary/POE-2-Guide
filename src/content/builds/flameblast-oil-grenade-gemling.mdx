## Summary

This Gemling Legionnaire channels <Item name="Flameblast" /> to build up a huge explosion, then ignites a pool of oil from <Item name="Oil Grenade" /> for even more damage. It's very tanky for a ranged build and almost a one-button character, which makes it a favourite for boss farming.

## Pros and cons

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Massive boss damage | Slower map clear than top speed builds |
| Simple, almost one-button | You stand still while channeling |
| Tanky Mercenary defenses | Scaling needs some investment |

## How the build works

Flameblast charges up to 10 stages while you channel, then releases one big fire explosion. Oil Grenade coats the ground and enemies, and fire ignites the oil for extra damage. The Gemling Legionnaire lets you run more support gems and raises gem quality, so every skill hits harder. Use <Item name="Elemental Weakness" /> to lower enemy resistances.

## Leveling

Level as a crossbow Mercenary with grenades and bolt skills. <Item name="Explosive Grenade" /> and <Item name="Gas Grenade" /> clear well early. Switch to Flameblast once you can socket it with a few supports.

## Ascendancy order

1. **Extra supports**: more support gems on Flameblast.
2. **Gem quality**: stronger gems across the board.
3. **Flexible attributes**: frees up the passive tree.
4. Take the remaining node that best fits your gear.

## Passive tree

Fire and area damage, spell and grenade clusters, armour and life. Plan with our [passive tree planner](/passive-tree/) or a current build on [poe.ninja](https://poe.ninja/poe2/builds).

## Gear

**Starter.** Any crossbow or staff that lets you use your skills, life and armour, and resistances.

**Mid.** Gear with +levels to skills, cast speed and fire damage. Cap resistances.

**Endgame.** Higher gem levels and fire penetration. Check [poe.ninja](https://poe.ninja/poe2/builds) for current item choices.

## Defenses

Mercenary armour and life, plus Gemling's flexible attributes letting you invest in defense. Cap resistances and keep a life flask. Since you stand still while channeling, learn boss attack timing.

## Playstyle

Throw Oil Grenade at the boss, place Artillery Ballista, curse with Elemental Weakness, then channel Flameblast to 10 stages and release. Dodge, then repeat.

## Atlas and farming pairing

Built for bosses. [Citadel fragment farming](/farming/citadel-fragments/) and the boss half of [Delirium and boss rush](/farming/delirium-boss-rush/) suit it best.

## Common mistakes

- **Releasing Flameblast early.** Most damage comes from full stages.
- **Forgetting the oil.** Oil Grenade is a big damage multiplier on bosses.
- **Channeling in danger.** Move first, then channel.

## Summary

The Plant Oracle, nicknamed "Plant Daddy" by the community, grows vines that hold enemies in place and crush them. It's cheap, tanky thanks to energy shield, and needs little precision. A budget version works with self-found gear.

## Pros and cons

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Cheap to start and to scale | Mana-hungry; needs mana sustain |
| Tanky energy shield defense | Chaos resistance is a must, and it's hard to get early |
| Low effort to play | Unusual setup if you've never used Cast on Critical |

## How the build works

<Item name="Entangle" /> roots enemies in vines, and <Item name="Thrashing Vines" /> makes them lash out for physical damage. The Oracle's Forced Outcome makes critical hits certain, which turns <Item name="Cast on Critical" /> into a reliable engine: every crit triggers extra spells like <Item name="Thunderstorm" />. <Item name="Mana Remnants" /> helps keep your mana topped up.

## Leveling

Level with simple Druid spells and pick up Entangle and Thrashing Vines as they become available. Switch to the Cast on Critical setup after the Oracle's Forced Outcome is allocated, since the build relies on guaranteed crits.

## Ascendancy order

1. **Forced outcome**: certain crits; the core of the build.
2. **Align fate**: visions that cast extra spells.
3. **Moment of vulnerability**: helps ailments and damage on bosses.
4. **The unseen path**: opens extra passive tree options.

## Passive tree

Energy shield, spell damage, physical spell damage and mana. Pick up chaos resistance where you can. Plan in our [passive tree planner](/passive-tree/) or import a current version from [poe.ninja](https://poe.ninja/poe2/builds).

## Gear

**Starter.** A <Item name="Chiming Staff" /> or any staff with spell damage, plus energy shield and resistances on armour.

**Mid.** Higher energy shield, +levels to spell gems, mana regeneration and chaos resistance.

**Endgame.** High energy shield, gem levels, and physical spell damage. Check [poe.ninja](https://poe.ninja/poe2/builds) for current unique choices.

## Defenses

Energy shield is your main buffer, and rooted enemies can't reach you. Cap elemental resistances and push chaos resistance toward positive numbers.

## Playstyle

Cast Entangle three times, then Thrashing Vines once. Repeat. Keep a mana flask ready until your sustain is solid.

## Atlas and farming pairing

The build is tanky enough for [Expedition](/farming/expedition/) and [Ritual](/farming/ritual/), where you stand and fight waves.

## Common mistakes

- **Running out of mana.** Get Mana Remnants and mana regeneration early.
- **Ignoring chaos resistance.** Energy shield doesn't protect against chaos damage as well as you'd hope.
- **Spamming Thrashing Vines alone.** It needs Entangle's roots to shine.

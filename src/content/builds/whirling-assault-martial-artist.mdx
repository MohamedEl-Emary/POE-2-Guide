## Summary

Whirling Assault Martial Artist spins through packs with a quarterstaff, building Combo and a stacking defensive buff the longer you fight. It was the most recommended melee starter in 0.5.5: cheap, tanky for melee, and strong on bosses.

## Pros and cons

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Best melee starter in the patch | Melee means you must get close to danger |
| Gets tankier the longer you fight | Needs some energy shield and evasion balance |
| Good single-target damage | Clear is a bit slower than top ranged builds |

## How the build works

<Item name="Whirling Assault" /> moves you forward while spinning, hitting everything around you. Hits build Combo, which powers your strongest skills. The Martial Artist's Mountain's Teachings buff stacks while you attack, adding damage and reducing the damage you take from smaller hits. Fists of Stone makes your gloves much stronger.

## Leveling

| Level | What to do |
|---|---|
| 1-8 | Quarterstaff strikes. Take <Item name="Falling Thunder" /> early for packs. |
| 8-20 | Add <Item name="Herald of Thunder" /> and <Item name="Tempest Bell" />. |
| 20-40 | Take <Item name="Whirling Assault" /> and <Item name="Killing Palm" /> (it collects power charges from weak enemies). |
| 40+ | Add <Item name="Charged Staff" />, <Item name="Mantra of Destruction" /> and the full supports below. |

## Ascendancy order

1. **Combo**: core damage and the reason Whirling Assault feels strong.
2. **Mountain's Teachings**: damage plus a big defensive boost.
3. **Fists of Stone**: turns your gloves into a major power source.
4. **Runic meridians**: extra rune sockets in your body armour.

## Passive tree

Take quarterstaff, lightning and attack speed clusters, then evasion and energy shield. Use our [passive tree planner](/passive-tree/) or a current build from [poe.ninja](https://poe.ninja/poe2/builds).

## Gear

**Starter.** The best quarterstaff you can find, plus life, resistances and movement speed.

**Mid.** Rare gloves with attack speed and added damage (they matter more because of Fists of Stone). Cap resistances. Mix evasion and energy shield on armour.

**Endgame.** High-damage quarterstaff with attack speed and critical hit chance, gloves with big flat damage, and higher evasion and energy shield totals.

## Defenses

Mountain's Teachings, <GlossaryTerm id="evasion" />, <GlossaryTerm id="energy-shield" /> with Ghost Dance, and capped resistances. Keep attacking: your defense is strongest mid-fight.

## Playstyle

Spin into packs with Whirling Assault. On bosses: place Tempest Bell, hit it, use Killing Palm on adds for charges, spend them with Charged Staff, and keep spinning.

## Atlas and farming pairing

Solid all-rounder. [Expedition](/farming/expedition/) and [Ritual](/farming/ritual/) both suit a tanky melee character.

## Common mistakes

- **Neglecting gloves.** With Fists of Stone, gloves are a weapon-level upgrade.
- **Standing in ground effects.** Melee builds should dodge roll out, then re-engage.
- **Using a poor quarterstaff.** Upgrade it before anything else.

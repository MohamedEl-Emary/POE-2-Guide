## Summary

Essence Drain Lich fires chaos projectiles that deal <GlossaryTerm id="damage-over-time" /> and heal you, while <Item name="Contagion" /> spreads that damage to every nearby enemy when something dies. It's one of the cheapest strong starters in 0.5.5.

## Pros and cons

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Very cheap; great with self-found gear | Damage over time takes a moment to kill |
| Chain-reacts through packs | Squishy until you're fully on energy shield |
| Comfortable from range | Bosses are slower than with burst builds |

## How the build works

Cast Contagion on a pack, then <Item name="Essence Drain" /> on one enemy. When an enemy with Contagion dies, it spreads Contagion and Essence Drain to others, so the whole pack melts. <Item name="Despair" /> curses bosses to take more chaos damage. The Lich adds energy shield strength and better curses.

## Leveling

| Level | What to do |
|---|---|
| 1-10 | Any Witch starting spell, then <Item name="Contagion" /> and <Item name="Essence Drain" /> as soon as possible. |
| 10-30 | Add <Item name="Despair" /> for bosses. |
| 30+ | Add <Item name="Blink" /> for mobility and follow the full setup. |

## Ascendancy order

1. **Soulless Form**
2. **Eternal Life**
3. **Eldritch Empowerment**: take it only once you're fully on energy shield.
4. **Crystalline Phylactery**

## Gear

**Starter.** Any wand or staff with spell or chaos damage, plus energy shield and resistances.

**Mid.** +levels to chaos spell gems, cast speed and energy shield. <Item name="Atziri's Disdain" unique /> is a popular helmet. Keep a <Item name="Chiming Staff" /> on weapon set 2.

**Endgame.** <Item name="Visage of Ayah" unique /> and high energy shield rares with gem levels. Check current prices on [poe.ninja](https://poe.ninja/poe2/economy).

## Defenses

Energy shield is the main pool. Stay at range, keep resistances capped, and blink away from danger.

## Playstyle

Contagion, then Essence Drain on one target, then move on while the pack dies. On bosses: Despair, Contagion, Essence Drain, then keep re-applying while dodging.

## Atlas and farming pairing

Good with [Expedition](/farming/expedition/) and [Abyss omen farming](/farming/abyss-omens/), where packs keep spreading damage.

## Common mistakes

- **Taking Eldritch Empowerment too early.** Wait until you're fully on energy shield.
- **Not re-casting Contagion.** Clear stalls if the chain breaks.
- **Low chaos resistance.** You deal chaos damage, but you also take it.

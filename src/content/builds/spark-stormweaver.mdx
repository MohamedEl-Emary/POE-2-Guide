## Summary

Spark Stormweaver throws streams of crackling sparks that bounce along the ground and walls, filling rooms with lightning. The Stormweaver adds storms and stacking <GlossaryTerm id="shock" />. It's a classic, easy-to-understand mage and a good starter for players who want a caster.

## Pros and cons

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Simple and satisfying | Squishy, especially early |
| Strong in corridors and packs | Weaker in wide-open rooms |
| Cheap to start | Mana sustain needs attention |

## How the build works

<Item name="Spark" /> fires many small projectiles that wander and hit enemies repeatedly. Shock makes enemies take more damage, and the Stormweaver stacks it higher. <Item name="Orb of Storms" /> adds more lightning and shock, and <Item name="Arc" /> helps on single targets.

## Leveling

| Level | What to do |
|---|---|
| 1-10 | <Item name="Spark" /> from the start. |
| 10-30 | Add <Item name="Orb of Storms" /> and <Item name="Arc" />. |
| 30+ | Add supports and curses as sockets allow. |

## Ascendancy order

1. **Stacking shock**: a big damage boost against everything.
2. **Elemental storms**: extra area damage.
3. **Critical power**
4. **Arcane bonuses**: smoother mana.

## Passive tree

Lightning damage, cast speed, projectile damage for spells, energy shield and mana. Use our [passive tree planner](/passive-tree/) or a current build from [poe.ninja](https://poe.ninja/poe2/builds).

## Gear

**Starter.** A wand or staff with lightning or spell damage, plus mana and resistances.

**Mid.** +levels to lightning spells, cast speed and energy shield.

**Endgame.** High gem levels and critical hit stats. Check [poe.ninja](https://poe.ninja/poe2/builds).

## Defenses

Energy shield, range and mobility. Cap resistances; this build can't afford to take hits.

## Playstyle

Spam Spark down corridors and into packs. On bosses: Orb of Storms first, then Spark from close range so more sparks hit.

## Atlas and farming pairing

[Breach](/farming/breach/) for packs, and with investment the [Delirium and boss rush](/farming/delirium-boss-rush/) setup.

## Common mistakes

- **Fighting at max range on bosses.** Sparks spread out; get a bit closer.
- **Low mana.** Add mana regeneration early.
- **Ignoring defense.** Energy shield and resistances first.

## Summary

The Disciple of Varashta summons three named djinn who fight for you, backed by permanent <GlossaryTerm id="minion">minions</GlossaryTerm>. Once set up, this is one of the safest builds in 0.5.5 because the monsters chase your allies instead of you. It's fragile early, so it isn't our first pick for a league start.

## Pros and cons

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Very safe once set up | Weak and squishy before the Ascendancy comes online |
| Djinn handle most of the work | Many Ascendancy choices to learn |
| Strong single-target | Spirit-hungry gear requirements |

## How the build works

Your Ascendancy unlocks three djinn: Ruzhan, Navira and Kelari, each with its own role. <Item name="Muster" /> gives your minions more damage for each unique type of permanent minion you have, so you run a mix of minions such as <Item name="Raging Spirits" /> and skeletons from <Item name="Unearth" />. <Item name="Living Lightning" /> adds extra damage.

## Leveling

Level as a Sorceress with elemental spells, adding minions as Spirit allows. Switch to the full djinn setup once you have your first Ascendancy points.

## Ascendancy order

1. **Barya of Kelari** and **Ruzhan**: your first djinn and the core of early damage.
2. **Navira** plus **Kelari's Deception**.
3. **The Fourth Teaching**.
4. **Sacred Rituals**, later swapped for **Baryanic Leylines**.

## Gear

**Starter.** Spirit, +minion skill levels and energy shield.

**Mid.** <Item name="Trenchtimbre" unique /> and <Item name="Pariah's Embrace" unique /> are popular. Keep resistances capped.

**Endgame.** More minion levels and Spirit for extra minion types.

## Defenses

Allies draw aggro. Energy shield and capped resistances cover the rest. Stay behind your djinn.

## Playstyle

Re-summon minions after deaths, keep Muster up, and reposition while your djinn fight. On bosses, stay out of range and let them work.

## Atlas and farming pairing

[Ritual](/farming/ritual/) and [Expedition](/farming/expedition/) both let your allies fight while you stay safe.

## Common mistakes

- **Playing it as a league starter without a plan.** It's weak early.
- **Running two of the same minion type.** Muster rewards variety.
- **Running out of Spirit.** Plan Spirit on gear and Act rewards (see [permanent bonuses](/campaign/permanent-bonuses/)).

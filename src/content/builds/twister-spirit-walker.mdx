## Summary

Twister Spirit Walker sends spinning whirlwinds across the screen that grow stronger as they pass through each other. It was one of the fastest mapping builds in 0.5.5. It starts on a reasonable budget, but the endgame version uses expensive uniques, and it asks you to juggle a few resources.

<Callout type="beginner">
  This is a great second character. If it's your very first, level with a simpler spear skill and switch to Twister once you're comfortable with weapon sets and buffs.
</Callout>

## Pros and cons

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Among the fastest clear speeds in the game | More buttons and buffs than most starters |
| Huge area coverage, little aiming | Boss damage is lower than pure single-target builds |
| Scales very well with investment | Best version needs pricey uniques |

## How the build works

Twister is a spear skill that releases whirlwinds which travel forward. When you charge other spear skills first, Twister picks up extra whirlwinds and elemental damage, so the rotation is "build up, then release". The Spirit Walker adds spirit animals: Vivid Stampede (a stag rush built from moving), Primal Bounty (owl feathers spent when you dodge) and Wild Protector (a bear that shields you).

## Leveling

Level as any Huntress spear build, using whatever spear skills feel good, and pick up <Item name="Twister" /> once you have room for its supports.

| Level | What to do |
|---|---|
| 1-12 | Spear attacks and <Item name="Whirling Slash" /> for packs. |
| 12-30 | Add <Item name="Twister" />. Use Whirling Slash to charge it, then release. |
| 30-50 | Add <Item name="Ice-Tipped Arrows" /> and <Item name="Barrage" /> to boost Twister further. |
| 50+ | Pick up <Item name="Wind Dancer" /> and <Item name="Ghost Dance" /> for defense, then follow the full setup. |

## Ascendancy order

1. **Primal Bounty**: feathers on dodge give you damage and recovery.
2. **Vivid Stampede**: a free stag stampede while you move through maps.
3. **Wild Protector**: a spirit bear that helps you survive.
4. **Tamed beasts**: rounds out the build; check a current guide for the best pick at your budget.

## Gear

**Starter.** A spear with high elemental or physical damage, life and resistances on everything else, and movement speed boots.

**Mid.** <Item name="Skysliver" unique /> and <Item name="Rite of Passage" unique /> are popular mid-budget uniques. Keep resistances at 75% and add evasion.

**Endgame.** <Item name="Headhunter" unique />, <Item name="The Taming" unique />, <Item name="Lavianga's Spirits" unique /> and <Item name="Heart of the Well" unique /> push clear and damage much higher. These are expensive; check prices on [poe.ninja](https://poe.ninja/poe2/economy) before planning around them.

## Defenses

Evasion, Wind Dancer and Ghost Dance form the base. The Wild Protector bear and moving fast do the rest. Keep resistances capped and don't stand in boss effects.

## Playstyle

Rotation: <Item name="Ice-Tipped Arrows" />, then <Item name="Whirling Slash" />, then <Item name="Twister" />. Keep moving so Vivid Stampede builds up, and dodge roll often to spend feathers.

## Atlas and farming pairing

Fast, wide clear makes this a natural fit for [Breach](/farming/breach/) and [Expedition](/farming/expedition/). Pinnacle bosses are possible but slower than with dedicated bossers.

## Common mistakes

- **Casting Twister without charging it.** Most of the damage comes from the buildup.
- **Ignoring defense for speed.** Fast builds die fast too; get Wind Dancer and capped resistances.
- **Buying Headhunter too early.** Get resistances, life and a good spear first.

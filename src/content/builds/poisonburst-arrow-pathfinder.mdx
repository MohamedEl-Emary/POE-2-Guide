## Summary

Poisonburst Arrow Pathfinder fires arrows that burst into clouds of <GlossaryTerm id="poison" />, which then spreads from enemy to enemy. The Pathfinder keeps you mobile and turns flasks into weapons. It's a solid alternative to Ice Shot for players who like damage over time.

## Pros and cons

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Poison spreads through packs | Poison takes time to kill |
| Very mobile | Boss damage is lower than top builds |
| Works with self-found gear | Several skills to layer |

## How the build works

<Item name="Poisonburst Arrow" /> poisons a target and bursts into a cloud. <Item name="Toxic Growth" />, <Item name="Vine Arrow" /> and <Item name="Gas Arrow" /> add more poison sources, and <Item name="Herald of Plague" /> spreads it further. The Pathfinder spreads poison on its own too. <Item name="Despair" /> boosts chaos damage on bosses.

## Leveling

Level with Ranger bow skills and add Poisonburst Arrow as soon as you can. Physical and chaos damage on your bow both help poison.

## Ascendancy order

1. **Running Assault**
2. **Relentless Pursuit**
3. **Sustainable Practices**
4. **Path Seeker**

## Gear

**Starter.** A bow with flat physical damage, life and resistances.

**Mid.** <Item name="Plaguefinger" unique /> gloves are a popular choice. Add evasion and cap resistances.

**Endgame.** Higher bow damage and poison stats. Check [poe.ninja](https://poe.ninja/poe2/builds) for current items.

## Defenses

Evasion, Wind Dancer, mobility and range. Keep life and resistances high.

## Playstyle

Fire Poisonburst Arrow into packs and keep moving. On bosses: Despair, Vine Arrow, Gas Arrow, then Poisonburst repeatedly.

## Atlas and farming pairing

[Abyss omen farming](/farming/abyss-omens/) and [Expedition](/farming/expedition/), where packs are dense.

## Common mistakes

- **Stacking only elemental damage.** Poison scales from physical and chaos.
- **Standing still waiting for poison.** Move on; it keeps ticking.

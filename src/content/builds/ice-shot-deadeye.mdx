## Summary

Ice Shot Deadeye shoots arrows that shatter into cones of ice shards, freezing and clearing whole packs from a safe distance. It needs no expensive items to work, plays well from level 1, and scales smoothly into high-tier maps. If you have never played Path of Exile before, this is the build we would hand you first.

## Pros and cons

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Works with any decent bow you find | You must keep moving; standing still gets you killed |
| Kills from far away, which is very forgiving | Evasion-based defenses feel shaky until your gear improves |
| Cheap and fully viable in <GlossaryTerm id="ssf" /> | Bosses take longer than packs until you add Snipe |
| Few buttons, simple rotation | Accuracy matters more than new players expect |

## How the build works

Ice Shot fires an arrow that hits the first enemy, then bursts into a cone of shards behind it. Those shards hit everything nearby, so one arrow damages a whole pack. Cold damage also slows and <GlossaryTerm id="freeze">freezes</GlossaryTerm> enemies, which is a defense in itself: frozen monsters can't hit you.

The Deadeye <GlossaryTerm id="ascendancy" /> adds extra arrows and a speed buff called Tailwind. More arrows means more shards, and Tailwind keeps you fast as long as you avoid getting hit. For bosses you add Snipe, a charged shot that deals a big single hit.

## Leveling

You can use Ice Shot almost from the start, since it's one of the first bow skills you can pick.

| Level | What to do |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | Use the starting bow attack. Grab <Item name="Lightning Arrow" /> from the first <GlossaryTerm id="uncut-gem">uncut gem</GlossaryTerm> if you find it before Ice Shot. |
| 3-8 | Take <Item name="Ice Shot" /> as soon as you can. Add <Item name="Rapid Attacks I" /> as its first support. |
| 8-20 | Add <Item name="Barrage" /> for tough single enemies and <Item name="Herald of Ice" /> once you have the <GlossaryTerm id="spirit" />. |
| 20-40 | Add <Item name="Snipe" /> for bosses and <Item name="Freezing Mark" /> to make big targets freeze faster. |
| 40+ | Switch to the full gem setup below as your sockets allow. |

<Callout type="tip">
  Always buy or keep the best bow you can find. In PoE2 a bow's base damage matters more than almost anything else for an attack build. Check vendors every town visit.
</Callout>

## Ascendancy order

1. **Projectile Proximity Specialisation**: more damage based on distance, perfect for a ranged build.
2. **Endless Munitions**: an extra arrow, which is a big damage and clear boost for Ice Shot.
3. **Gathering Winds**: unlocks Tailwind for speed and evasion.
4. **Mirage Deadeye**: adds a mirage that fires alongside you.

See [Ascendancy trials](/campaign/ascendancy-trials/) for when you can earn each pair of points.

## Gear

**Starter (campaign to early maps).** Any bow with high physical or cold damage. Fill the rest with life, resistances and accuracy. Put a <Item name="Greater Iron Rune" /> (or a lower Iron Rune) in your bow socket for extra damage. Buy boots with movement speed early.

**Mid (tier 10-15 maps).** A rare <Item name="Warmonger Bow" /> or <Item name="Obliterator Bow" /> with added damage and attack speed. Cap your fire, cold and lightning <GlossaryTerm id="resistances" /> at 75%, then work on chaos resistance. Aim for 1,500+ life.

**Endgame.** Higher-tier bows with critical hit chance, quiver with projectile damage and +arrows, and eventually <Item name="Headhunter" unique /> for huge clear speed if your budget allows.

| Stat priority | Why |
|---|---|
| Bow damage | Every hit scales from it |
| Resistances (75%) | Biggest defensive gain per item slot |
| Life and evasion | Keeps you alive when something reaches you |
| Accuracy | Missing is lost damage |

## Defenses

You rely on distance, freeze and <GlossaryTerm id="evasion" />. Wind Dancer adds evasion when you're not being hit. Keep your resistances capped, use the <GlossaryTerm id="dodge-roll" /> whenever a boss winds up a big attack, and keep a life <GlossaryTerm id="flask" /> ready.

## Playstyle

Shoot packs as you move into them, and let shards hit everything behind the first target. On bosses: place Freezing Mark, fire Barrage-empowered Ice Shots, then charge Snipe when the boss is frozen or busy. Never stand in one spot for long.

## Atlas and farming pairing

Ice Shot's fast, safe clear suits mechanics with lots of monsters. [Breach](/farming/breach/) and [Expedition](/farming/expedition/) both fit well, and the build is strong enough to start boss fragment farming once you add Snipe.

## Common mistakes

- **Keeping a weak bow.** Upgrade your bow more often than any other item.
- **Skipping accuracy.** If many arrows miss, add accuracy on gloves, rings or the tree.
- **Uncapped resistances in maps.** You lose 10% elemental resistance after each act; fix it before mapping.
- **Standing still to Snipe.** Only charge Snipe when the boss can't punish you.

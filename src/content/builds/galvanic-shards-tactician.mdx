## Summary

Galvanic Shards Tactician fires lightning bolts from a crossbow that chain through packs, while ballistas and a tamed beast add extra damage. The Tactician pins enemies in place so they can't hit back. It clears fast and has good boss damage with Shockburst Rounds.

## Pros and cons

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Very fast clear | Several skills to manage |
| Strong boss damage | Needs at least 64 Spirit |
| Pins stop enemies from fighting back | Evasion defense can feel thin early |

## How the build works

<Item name="Galvanic Shards" /> fires lightning shards that spread across packs. <Item name="Shockburst Rounds" /> deal heavy single-target damage. <Item name="Artillery Ballista" /> and a beast from <Item name="Tame Beast" /> add damage you don't aim. The Tactician pins enemies and drops banners for bonuses. <Item name="Mirage Archer" /> and <Item name="Emergency Reload" /> boost your attacks further.

## Leveling

Level with crossbow skills such as <Item name="Fragmentation Rounds" /> and <Item name="Permafrost Bolts" />, then switch to Galvanic Shards once you can support it.

## Gear

**Starter.** The best crossbow you can find, life, resistances and Spirit.

**Mid.** <Item name="Desolate Crossbow" /> or <Item name="Gemini Crossbow" /> bases, <Item name="Breach Ring" />s and a <Item name="Solar Amulet" />.

**Endgame.** <Item name="Maligaro's Virtuosity" unique /> gloves and a high-end crossbow. Check [poe.ninja](https://poe.ninja/poe2/economy) before buying.

## Defenses

Evasion, pins and distance. Keep resistances capped and use dodge roll freely.

## Playstyle

Clear with Galvanic Shards. On bosses: place ballistas, use Emergency Reload, then fire Shockburst Rounds while dodging.

## Atlas and farming pairing

[Breach](/farming/breach/) and [Abyss omen farming](/farming/abyss-omens/) suit its fast chaining clear.

## Common mistakes

- **Not enough Spirit.** The build needs at least 64.
- **Ignoring the beast.** Your tamed beast is real damage.
- **Skipping evasion.** Pins are great, but you still need a defensive layer.

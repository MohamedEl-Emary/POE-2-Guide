Crafting in Path of Exile 2 means using **currency items** to change the modifiers on your gear. There's no crafting bench full of recipes. Instead, each orb does one specific thing, and you combine them to shape an item.

<Callout type="beginner">
  During the campaign, the best "crafting" is simply picking up items and equipping upgrades. Save your valuable currency for trading until you understand what it's worth.
</Callout>

## Item rarity

Every item has a <GlossaryTerm id="item-rarity" />:

| Rarity | Colour | Modifiers |
|---|---|---|
| Normal | White | None |
| Magic | Blue | Up to 2 |
| Rare | Yellow | Up to 6 |
| Unique | Orange | Fixed, special modifiers |

Crafting mostly happens on **Rare** items, because they can hold the most modifiers.

## Modifiers

A <GlossaryTerm id="modifier" /> is one line of stats, like "+30 to maximum Life". Rare items can have up to three **prefixes** and three **suffixes** (see <GlossaryTerm id="prefix-suffix" />). Modifiers come in tiers, and higher tiers have bigger numbers.

## The basic path from Normal to Rare

1. <GlossaryTerm id="orb-of-transmutation" />: Normal → Magic, adds one modifier.
2. <GlossaryTerm id="orb-of-augmentation" />: adds a second modifier to the Magic item.
3. <GlossaryTerm id="regal-orb" />: Magic → Rare, adds a third modifier.
4. <GlossaryTerm id="exalted-orb" />: adds another modifier to a Rare item, up to six.

Or skip straight to Rare with an <GlossaryTerm id="orb-of-alchemy" />.

Transmutation, Augmentation, Regal, Chaos and Exalted Orbs also come in **Greater** and **Perfect** versions, such as <Item name="Greater Exalted Orb" /> and <Item name="Perfect Exalted Orb" />, which guarantee higher-tier modifiers.

## Changing an item that's already Rare

- <GlossaryTerm id="chaos-orb" />: swaps one random modifier for a new one.
- <GlossaryTerm id="orb-of-annulment" />: removes one random modifier.
- <GlossaryTerm id="divine-orb" />: rerolls the numbers on the existing modifiers.

<Callout type="warning" title="Coming from Path of Exile 1?">
  There's no Orb of Scouring and no Orb of Alteration in PoE2, and the Chaos Orb only changes one modifier rather than the whole item.
</Callout>

## Tools for more control

- <GlossaryTerm id="essence">Essences</GlossaryTerm> add a guaranteed modifier. See [Essences](/crafting/essences/).
- <GlossaryTerm id="omen">Omens</GlossaryTerm> change how your next orb behaves. See [Omens](/crafting/omens/).
- <GlossaryTerm id="catalyst">Catalysts</GlossaryTerm> add quality to rings and amulets, for example <Item name="Flesh Catalyst" />.
- <GlossaryTerm id="corruption" /> with a <GlossaryTerm id="vaal-orb" /> is a final gamble that locks the item.
- Desecrated modifiers from Abyss add hidden modifiers that are revealed later.

## Should you craft at all?

For most new players: **not much, at first**. Random crafting with valuable orbs is usually worse than buying a finished item. Good beginner crafts are cheap, targeted ones, covered in [Crafting strategies](/crafting/strategies/).

In Path of Exile 2, currency is both money and crafting material. This page explains what each common item does. Hover, tap or tab to any item to see its tooltip. For what each one is **worth**, always check live prices on poe.ninja. Values change daily.

<CurrencyGrid />

These are the everyday ones. See [every currency and crafting item](/items/currency/) for the full list, including essences, omens, runes and league currency.

## Crafting orbs

| Currency | What it does |
|---|---|
| <Item name="Orb of Transmutation" /> | Normal → Magic with one modifier |
| <Item name="Orb of Augmentation" /> | Adds a modifier to a Magic item |
| <Item name="Regal Orb" /> | Magic → Rare, adding a modifier |
| <Item name="Orb of Alchemy" /> | Normal → Rare with random modifiers |
| <Item name="Exalted Orb" /> | Adds a random modifier to a Rare item |
| <Item name="Chaos Orb" /> | Replaces one random modifier on a Rare item |
| <Item name="Orb of Annulment" /> | Removes one random modifier |
| <Item name="Divine Orb" /> | Rerolls the values of an item's modifiers |
| <Item name="Vaal Orb" /> | Corrupts an item with an unpredictable result |
| <Item name="Orb of Chance" /> | Gives a Normal item a small chance to become a Unique |
| <Item name="Fracturing Orb" /> | Locks one modifier permanently in place |
| <Item name="Mirror of Kalandra" /> | Creates a copy of an item (extremely rare) |

**Greater** and **Perfect** versions of Transmutation, Augmentation, Regal, Chaos and Exalted Orbs exist. They guarantee higher-tier modifiers and are worth much more.

## Quality and socket items

| Currency | What it does |
|---|---|
| <Item name="Blacksmith's Whetstone" /> | Adds quality to weapons |
| <Item name="Armourer's Scrap" /> | Adds quality to armour |
| <Item name="Arcanist's Etcher" /> | Adds quality to wands, staves and other caster weapons |
| <Item name="Glassblower's Bauble" /> | Adds quality to flasks |
| <Item name="Gemcutter's Prism" /> | Adds quality to gems |
| <GlossaryTerm id="catalyst">Catalysts</GlossaryTerm> | Add quality to rings and amulets, boosting a type of modifier |
| <Item name="Artificer's Orb" /> | Adds a rune socket to equipment |
| <Item name="Lesser Jeweller's Orb">Jeweller's Orbs</Item> | Add support gem sockets to a skill gem |

## Everyday utility

- <Item name="Scroll of Wisdom" />: identifies an unidentified item.
- <GlossaryTerm id="gold" />: spent at vendors and on refunding passive points. It can't be traded.

## Which currencies matter for trading

Two currencies set most prices:

- <GlossaryTerm id="exalted-orb">Exalted Orbs</GlossaryTerm> for everyday purchases.
- <GlossaryTerm id="divine-orb">Divine Orbs</GlossaryTerm> for expensive items.

Smaller trades are sometimes priced in Chaos Orbs. Convert between them on the <GlossaryTerm id="currency-exchange" />.

<Callout type="tip">
  Don't use your Exalted Orbs on random crafts while leveling. They're far more useful for buying gear that's already good.
</Callout>

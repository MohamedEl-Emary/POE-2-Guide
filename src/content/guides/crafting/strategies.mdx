These methods use cheap currency, give predictable results, and won't ruin your economy if they don't work out.

<Callout type="beginner">
  The golden rule: craft with cheap currency, trade with valuable currency. If an orb is worth a lot on poe.ninja, sell it instead of gambling with it.
</Callout>

## 1. Rolling a quick magic upgrade

Good for weapons while leveling, boots with movement speed and flasks.

1. Pick a Normal item with a good base for your level.
2. Use an <GlossaryTerm id="orb-of-transmutation" />, then an <GlossaryTerm id="orb-of-augmentation" />.
3. If both modifiers are useful, equip it. If not, try another base.

## 2. Fixing resistances with Essences

Good for rings, belts and boots when your <GlossaryTerm id="resistances" /> are low.

1. Find a fresh item with a good base for an empty or weak slot.
2. Make it Magic with an <GlossaryTerm id="orb-of-transmutation" />.
3. Use a resistance <GlossaryTerm id="essence" /> to turn it Rare with the resistance you're missing guaranteed.
4. Equip it if the other modifiers don't hurt you.

## 3. Turning a good Magic item into a Rare

Good when a Magic item already has two great modifiers.

1. Use a <GlossaryTerm id="regal-orb" /> to make it Rare. You keep the two good lines and get a third.
2. Only add <GlossaryTerm id="exalted-orb">Exalted Orbs</GlossaryTerm> if the item is already a real upgrade and Exalts are cheap for you.

## 4. Quality for easy free stats

Use quality currency like Armourer's Scrap and Blacksmith's Whetstone on gear you'll keep for a while. It's cheap and always helps.

## What not to do

- Don't spam Chaos or Exalted Orbs on random items hoping for something great.
- Don't use a <GlossaryTerm id="vaal-orb" /> on an item you can't afford to lose.
- Don't use an <GlossaryTerm id="orb-of-annulment" /> unless you know exactly why. It removes a random modifier.

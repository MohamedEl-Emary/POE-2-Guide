An <GlossaryTerm id="essence" /> is a crafting item that adds a **guaranteed specific modifier** to an item. Where most orbs are a gamble, an Essence lets you choose at least one thing you'll get.

## Where Essences come from

You'll find monsters **trapped in crystal** throughout the campaign and maps. Free them and kill them to get their Essence. They're common, and you'll collect plenty without trying.

## How they work

Each Essence is tied to a type of modifier, such as life, a resistance or a damage type. Higher tiers of the same Essence give stronger versions of that modifier. For example, <Item name="Essence of the Body" /> adds life to armour and jewellery, and <Item name="Essence of Flames" /> adds fire damage to a weapon. Hover or tap one to see exactly what it adds to each kind of item, or browse [every Essence](/items/currency/#essences).

Most Essences are used on a **Magic** item: they upgrade it to Rare and guarantee their modifier as one of the lines. The strongest tier works on Rare items instead, swapping out a random modifier for the guaranteed one. The rules can differ by tier, so read the Essence's description in game before you use it.

## Why they're great for beginners

- **Predictable:** you know you'll get the stat you need.
- **Cheap:** lower-tier Essences are plentiful.
- **Targeted:** perfect for fixing a specific problem, like low fire resistance.

<Callout type="tip">
  Missing resistances? Make a fresh ring, belt or pair of boots Magic, then use a resistance Essence on it. It's one of the cheapest ways to fix your defences.
</Callout>

## When to sell them instead

Higher-tier Essences and rare types can be valuable. If you're not going to use one soon, check its price and consider selling it on the <GlossaryTerm id="currency-exchange" />.

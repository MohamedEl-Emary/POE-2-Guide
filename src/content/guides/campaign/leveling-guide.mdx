This guide takes you from level 1 to the Atlas. It isn't a speedrun route. It's the path that gets a new player through the campaign without getting stuck, while picking up every [permanent bonus](/campaign/permanent-bonuses/) on the way.

<Callout type="warning" title="Written for 0.5.5">
  This route covers Acts 1 to 4 and Interludes I to III. At 1.0 the campaign becomes six full Acts, and this page will be rewritten.
</Callout>

## Habits that make leveling faster

- **Upgrade your weapon often.** Check vendors at every town visit. For attack builds, a new weapon is the biggest damage jump available.
- **Keep your main skill's gem level up.** Use <Item name="Uncut Skill Gem" />s on the skills you use every fight, not on new skills you're curious about.
- **Buy movement speed boots.** Look for 10% early, then 15%, 20% and 25% as you level. Speed saves more time than anything else.
- **Upgrade flasks.** Vendors sell stronger flasks around levels 4, 10, 20, 30, 45 and 60.
- **Search vendors for resistances.** Paste `cold resistance|lightning resistance|fire resistance` into a vendor's search box to highlight useful items.
- **Don't spend crafting currency.** Save <Item name="Exalted Orb" />s and other orbs for trading or later crafts. See [crafting basics](/crafting/).
- **Use a <GlossaryTerm id="loot-filter" />.** It hides junk so you stop picking up everything.

## Act 1 (levels 1 to 14)

Act 1 teaches the basics. Take your time here; it's where you learn to dodge roll and read enemy attacks.

1. Follow the story to Clearfell and Beira (cold resistance).
2. Hunting Grounds: kill Crowbell for 2 passive points.
3. Freythorn: defeat the King in the Mists for 30 Spirit. You can use it on your first persistent buff.
4. Ogham Farmlands: find Una's Lute for 2 passive points.
5. Ogham Manor: Candlemass gives 20 life.
6. Finish the Act by defeating Count Geonor.

<Callout type="beginner">
  Spirit is a resource for permanent buffs and minions. When you get your first 30, look for a buff skill like a Herald that fits your build.
</Callout>

## Act 2 (levels 14 to 29)

The desert caravan act. Your big goal here is your first [Ascendancy trial](/campaign/ascendancy-trials/).

1. Get your first <Item name="Djinn Barya" /> and run the Trial of the Sekhemas for your first two Ascendancy points as soon as you can.
2. Keth: Kabala for 2 passive points.
3. Deshar: the Final Letter to Shambrin for 2 passive points.
4. Spires of Deshar: the Sisters of Garukhan shrine for lightning resistance.
5. Valley of the Titans: choose a relic bonus.
6. Defeat Jamanra in the Dreadnought to finish the Act.

## Act 3 (levels 30 to 42)

Jungles, swamps and Vaal ruins. Look for the Fate of the Vaal beacons that patch 0.5 added to help navigation.

1. Jungle Ruins: Mighty Silverfist for 2 passive points.
2. Azak Bog: Ignagduk for 30 Spirit.
3. Jiquani's Machinarium: use a soul core found there to reach Blackjaw for fire resistance.
4. Venom Crypts: choose a permanent bonus.
5. Run the Trial of Chaos with your first <Item name="Inscribed Ultimatum" /> for Ascendancy points 3 and 4.
6. Aggorat: the Sacrificial Heart for 2 passive points.

## Act 4 (levels 42 to 52)

You sail between Karui islands from Kingsmarch. You can visit most islands in any order, so start with the ones your character finds easiest.

1. Journey's End: Omniphobia for 2 passive points.
2. Find and defeat Yama the White for 2 passive points.
3. The Eye of Hinekora for 5% maximum mana.
4. The Halls of the Dead: choose attributes or resistance.
5. The Goddess of Justice: choose a flask bonus.

<Callout type="tip">
  By the end of Act 4 your resistances have dropped a lot. Spend a few minutes swapping in gear with resistances before the Interludes.
</Callout>

## Interludes I to III (levels 52 to 60+)

Three shorter chapters that lead you to the Atlas.

- Akthi and Anundr: 2 passive points.
- Skullmaw Stairway: 5% maximum life.
- Qimah: the Seven Pillars boon.
- Kriar Village: Lythara for 40 Spirit.
- The Abominable Yeti and Oswin: 2 passive points each.
- Elder Madox: a free unique item.
- Back in Kingsmarch: the Hooded One for 2 passive points.

When you finish Interlude III, talk to Doryani at the Vaal Ziggurat to open the Atlas. Continue with [the endgame](/endgame/).

## Common leveling mistakes

- **Skipping side areas with permanent rewards.** Missing 2 passive points here and there adds up.
- **Running six different skills at low gem levels.** Focus your uncut gems on one main skill.
- **Ignoring resistances until maps.** Fix them as you go, especially after Act 4.
- **Using an old weapon.** Replace it whenever you find something clearly better.

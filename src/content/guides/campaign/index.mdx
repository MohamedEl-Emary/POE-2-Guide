The campaign is the story part of Path of Exile 2. You play through a series of <GlossaryTerm id="act">Acts</GlossaryTerm>, each a chain of connected zones with a boss at the end. When you finish it, the endgame opens. Most players reach maps somewhere between level 60 and 65.

<Callout type="warning" title="Changing at 1.0">
  In patch 0.5.5 the campaign is Acts 1 to 4 followed by three Interludes. At 1.0 on December 11, 2026 it becomes six full Acts and the Interludes are retired. This section will be rewritten for that.
</Callout>

## How the campaign is laid out

| Part | Rough levels | Highlights |
|---|---|---|
| Act 1 | 1 to 14 | Learn the basics; final boss Count Geonor |
| Act 2 | 14 to 29 | Desert caravan; your first [Ascendancy trial](/campaign/ascendancy-trials/) |
| Act 3 | 30 to 42 | Jungle and Vaal ruins; the Trial of Chaos opens |
| Act 4 | 42 to 52 | Island-hopping from Kingsmarch, in any order you like |
| Interludes I to III | 52 to 60+ | Three shorter chapters that lead to the Atlas |

The level ranges are typical, not rules. If a boss feels too hard, the fix is usually gear or a few levels, not a new build.

## What to focus on

1. **Your main skill and its supports.** Skill gem level is a large share of your damage. Use new <GlossaryTerm id="uncut-gem">uncut gems</GlossaryTerm> to upgrade the skills you actually use.
2. **Your weapon.** For attack builds, the weapon matters more than any other slot.
3. **<GlossaryTerm id="resistances">Resistances</GlossaryTerm>.** Each Act completion lowers your elemental resistances. By maps you need gear to make up the difference.
4. **Permanent rewards.** Some quests give passive points, <GlossaryTerm id="spirit" /> or resistances that last forever. See [permanent bonuses](/campaign/permanent-bonuses/).
5. **Ascendancy points.** You get your first two in Act 2. See [Ascendancy trials](/campaign/ascendancy-trials/).

<Callout type="tip">
  Patch 0.5 added signposts and glowing trails that point toward the next objective. If you get lost, look for them before checking a guide.
</Callout>

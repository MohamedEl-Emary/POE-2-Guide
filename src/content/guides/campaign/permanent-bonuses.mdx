Some campaign quests give rewards that last for the life of your character: extra passive points, <GlossaryTerm id="spirit" />, life, mana or <GlossaryTerm id="resistances" />. Missing them makes your character weaker for no reason. Across the campaign they add up to **about 24 passive points and 100 Spirit**.

<Callout type="tip">
  A quick check: open your passive tree and compare your total points against your level. If you have fewer than expected, you skipped a reward. Most can still be collected by going back.
</Callout>

## Which choices to pick

- **Venom Crypts:** take the stun threshold for melee, elemental ailment threshold if you get frozen or shocked a lot, mana regeneration for casters who run out of mana.
- **Halls of the Dead:** take resistance if you're short on resistances, attributes if you're missing gem requirements.
- **Valley of the Titans relics:** these can be swapped later, so pick what helps now.

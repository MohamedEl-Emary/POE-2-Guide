Your <GlossaryTerm id="ascendancy" /> is your character's specialisation. You unlock it by completing a trial, and each completed trial gives two Ascendancy points. There are **eight points in total**, earned in four steps.

## The two trials

| Trial | Where it opens | Entry item | Style |
|---|---|---|---|
| <GlossaryTerm id="trial-of-the-sekhemas" /> | Act 2 | <Item name="Djinn Barya" /> | A run of rooms with an "honour" bar that drops when you're hit |
| <GlossaryTerm id="trial-of-chaos" /> | Act 3 | <Item name="Inscribed Ultimatum" /> | A series of rooms with challenges; you pick modifiers as you go |

You can earn every set of points from either trial. Most players use whichever they find easier for their build. Melee and tanky characters often prefer the Trial of Chaos; fast ranged builds often like the Sekhemas.

## When each pair of points unlocks

| Points | Requirement |
|---|---|
| 1 and 2 | Complete the first trial in Act 2 (Sekhemas) or Act 3 (Chaos). |
| 3 and 4 | Complete the next trial, usually the Trial of Chaos in Act 3. |
| 5 and 6 | Use a level 60+ Barya or Ultimatum, usually early in maps. |
| 7 and 8 | Use a level 75+ Barya in the Sekhemas, or a level 65+ Ultimatum in the Trial of Chaos. |

<Callout type="beginner">
  Do your first trial as soon as it opens in Act 2. Your first two Ascendancy points are among the biggest power boosts in the whole campaign.
</Callout>

## Where to get the entry items

- **First Djinn Barya:** from the campaign in Act 2. You'll get it from a boss in the desert (Balbala in the Traitor's Passage or the Watchful Twins, depending on your route).
- **First Inscribed Ultimatum:** from the campaign in Act 3.
- **Later ones:** both drop in maps. You can also buy them from other players. Higher-level versions give the later points.

## Tips for the Trial of the Sekhemas

- Honour works like a second health bar. Getting hit lowers it; reaching zero ends the run.
- Take boons that restore honour or reduce the damage you take.
- Avoid afflictions that make you slower or lower your defenses.

## Tips for the Trial of Chaos

- You choose modifiers between rooms. Pick ones your build can ignore; avoid ones that shut down your defense.
- Patch 0.5.5 lets you pause between rooms and gives rewards per room.

These are the community and official tools most players use. They're all external sites. We link to them, but we don't copy their data, and we're not affiliated with any of them.

## Prices and popular builds

- **[poe.ninja](https://poe.ninja/poe2)**: live prices for currency and unique items, plus statistics on which builds players are using. Check here before you trust any price.

## Trading

- **[Official trade site](https://www.pathofexile.com/trade2)**: search for items listed by other players. See [Trading basics](/start-here/trading/).

## Build planning

- **[Path of Building (PoE2)](https://pathofbuilding.community/)**: a free tool to plan your passive tree, gear and skills and see your character's stats. Many build guides share a PoB link. See <GlossaryTerm id="path-of-building" />.

## Game data

- **[poe2db.tw](https://poe2db.tw/)**: a detailed database of items, modifiers, skills and more.
- **[poe2wiki.net](https://www.poe2wiki.net/)**: the community wiki for Path of Exile 2.

## Loot filters

- **[FilterBlade](https://www.filterblade.xyz/)**: customise and sync NeverSink's loot filter to your account. See [Loot filters](/start-here/loot-filters/).

## Official news

- **[Official forum and announcements](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum)**: patch notes, league announcements and developer news.

<Callout type="tip">
  When a new patch arrives, these tools update over the first few days. If a price or build looks strange right after a patch, give it a day.
</Callout>

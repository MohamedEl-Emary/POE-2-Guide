The **Atlas of Worlds** is the endgame map in Path of Exile 2. It's huge and expands as you explore. Each node on it is a map you can run, and the Atlas is where you'll spend most of your time after the campaign.

## Waystones

A <GlossaryTerm id="waystone" /> is a consumable item that opens one map node. Put it in the device, travel in, clear the map, and the node is completed.

- **Tier** decides difficulty. Higher tiers mean tougher monsters and better rewards.
- **Modifiers** on a Waystone make the map more dangerous but increase its rewards. Read them before you run the map.
- Waystones drop from maps, so a healthy character keeps finding more.

<Callout type="tip">
  Running out of Waystones usually means you're not clearing enough of each map or not killing the map boss. Use a loot filter that highlights Waystones.
</Callout>

## Moving across the Atlas

You can only open nodes next to ones you've already completed, so you push outward step by step. Some nodes hold special content, like bosses, towers or mechanics, and are worth heading toward.

## Precursor Towers and tablets

<GlossaryTerm id="precursor-tower">Precursor Towers</GlossaryTerm> are special maps. Once you've completed one, you can slot <GlossaryTerm id="precursor-tablet">Precursor Tablets</GlossaryTerm> into it. Tablets add a mechanic or bonus to maps within the tower's range.

This is the heart of endgame strategy: choose where you play, place towers' tablets to boost those maps, and run them. Stacking several tablets around the same maps is a big part of <GlossaryTerm id="juicing" />.

## Death in maps

Dying in a map is costly. It sets back your experience progress and can end your attempt at that map. That's why defences matter more in the endgame than in the campaign.

## A safe way to progress

1. Run each tier until you're comfortable, then move up.
2. Keep your <GlossaryTerm id="resistances" /> capped. Higher tiers punish missing resistances hard.
3. Upgrade your gear before you upgrade your Waystones.
4. Add tablets and Waystone modifiers gradually.

The <GlossaryTerm id="atlas-passive-tree" /> is a second passive tree that changes the endgame instead of your character. Its nodes add mechanics to maps, increase rewards, or make specific content more common. In patch 0.5 it was rebuilt around the **Precursor Fortress**, which is now the only place to earn Atlas points.

## How you earn Atlas points

| Source | Points |
|---|---|
| Completing a Precursor Fortress map | +1 |
| Completing a Precursor Fortress tower | +3 |
| Total available in 0.5.5 | 311 |

You won't get all 311 quickly, and you don't need to. The first few dozen points already make a big difference.

## Revealing the Fortress

1. **Complete your first <GlossaryTerm id="precursor-tower" />** on the Atlas. This reveals part of the Precursor Fortress.
2. **Run Fortress maps.** Each one gives an Atlas point and pushes the Fortress outward.
3. **Defeat the Arbiter of Ash.** Collect the three <GlossaryTerm id="pinnacle-boss" /> fragments (<Item name="Weathered Crisis Fragment" />, <Item name="Faded Crisis Fragment" /> and <Item name="Ancient Crisis Fragment" />), then use them at the Burning Monolith.
4. **Start Origins of Divinity.** Kill the bosses of the Matriarch Hall (Phya) and the Patriarch Hall (Phyx). They drop an <Item name="Origin Cradle" /> and an <Item name="Origin Spark" />, which combine into an <Item name="Origin Core" />.
5. **Defeat the quest Arbiter of Divinity** in the Origin Tower. This doesn't reveal the whole Fortress yet.
6. **Do one more non-quest Arbiter of Divinity kill.** Clear one of each hall again for a new core, then kill the Arbiter. In 0.5.5 this reveals the entire Fortress.

<Callout type="warning" title="Older guides are wrong here">
  Guides written before 0.5.5 say you need about five Arbiter kills to reveal the Fortress. Patch 0.5.5 cut that to one extra kill after the quest version.
</Callout>

## Changing nodes

Many nodes in 0.5 offer a choice between options. Patch notes for 0.5 say you can switch these choices freely, so you don't need to respec to change them. Regular point allocations are a bigger commitment, so plan a little before spending.

## What to take first

- **Waystone sustain.** Anything that helps Waystones drop more often. Running out of maps stalls everything.
- **One mechanic.** Pick the mechanic you want to farm (see [farming](/farming/)) and take its nodes. Spreading points across many mechanics makes each weaker.
- **Tablet and tower nodes** once you start placing <GlossaryTerm id="precursor-tablet">Precursor Tablets</GlossaryTerm>.

<Callout type="beginner">
  Don't copy a 300-point tree from a guide when you only have 20 points. Start with Waystone sustain and one mechanic, then grow from there.
</Callout>

The Arbiter of Divinity was added in 0.5 and is the top pinnacle boss on the Atlas. Killing it is also how you fully reveal the Precursor Fortress.

## How to reach it

1. Find the Matriarch Hall and the Patriarch Hall in the Precursor Fortress.
2. Defeat Phya in the Matriarch Hall for an <Item name="Origin Cradle" />, and Phyx in the Patriarch Hall for an <Item name="Origin Spark" />.
3. Combine them into an <Item name="Origin Core" />.
4. Use the core in the Origin Tower to fight the Arbiter.

## Quest kill vs repeatable kills

| Kill | What it does |
|---|---|
| First (quest) kill | Completes the Origins of Divinity quest. Doesn't reveal the whole Fortress. |
| Second kill (first non-quest) | Reveals the entire Fortress in 0.5.5. |
| Later kills | Farmable for rewards. Each needs a new Origin Core. |

<Callout type="warning" title="Changed in 0.5.5">
  Older guides say about five kills are needed to reveal the Fortress. Since 0.5.5 it's one extra kill after the quest version.
</Callout>

## Before you go in

- This is the hardest fight on the Atlas. Aim for capped resistances, positive chaos resistance and a large life or energy shield pool.
- Practise on the [Arbiter of Ash](/endgame/bosses/arbiter-of-ash/) first.
- Bring your best single-target setup and swap out pure clear skills.

## Fight tips

- Learn one phase at a time. Dying while learning is normal.
- Prioritise dodging over damage; a dead character deals no damage.
- Watch for attacks that cover most of the arena, and save your dodge roll for them.

You met the King in the Mists in Act 1. In the endgame he returns through [Ritual](/endgame/mechanics/ritual/) as the gateway to the Ritual pinnacle.

## How to reach it

1. Run maps with Ritual altars and complete Ritual encounters.
2. The endgame King in the Mists drops The Head of the King.
3. The Head starts the Rite of the Nameless in Caer Tarth, which leads to the Ritual pinnacle key.

## Before you go in

- The King hits hard with single big attacks. Your life or energy shield pool matters.
- Strong single-target damage is a must. Boss farming him can be very profitable, but only if you kill him quickly.

## Fight tips

- He vanishes into mist and reappears. Don't chase blindly; wait for him to show.
- Save your dodge roll for his large slams.

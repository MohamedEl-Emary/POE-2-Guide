<GlossaryTerm id="pinnacle-boss">Pinnacle bosses</GlossaryTerm> are the hardest fights in the endgame. Each is tied to a mechanic or to the Atlas itself. Most have a **quest version** you do once and a **repeatable version** you can farm.

<Callout type="beginner">
  You don't need to fight pinnacle bosses to enjoy the endgame. Many players earn more by farming maps. Try them once your build feels strong in tier 15 maps.
</Callout>

## Which boss to try first

1. **Arbiter of Ash.** You'll meet it naturally while earning Atlas points.
2. **The King in the Mists.** You already beat a version in Act 1, so the fight will feel familiar.
3. **Simulacrum.** Its waves test clear and defense more than single-target damage.

## Preparing for a boss

- Cap your resistances at 75% and add chaos resistance.
- Check that your build has a single-target skill, not just pack clear.
- Read the boss's big attacks first and practise dodging them.
- Bring flasks that cover your weaknesses.

Prices for boss entry items move constantly. Check [poe.ninja](https://poe.ninja/poe2/economy) before buying fragments.

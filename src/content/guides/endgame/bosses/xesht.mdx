Xesht is the pinnacle boss of [Breach](/endgame/mechanics/breach/). You reach it by progressing through Breach content on the Atlas.

## How to reach it

1. Run maps with Breach and collect <Item name="Breach Splinter" />s.
2. Combine splinters into a <Item name="Breachstone" />.
3. Progress through the Breach bosses (Tul and Esh) to earn access to Xesht.

## Before you go in

- Breach monsters hit hard and fast. Cap resistances and bring strong single-target damage.
- Clear speed doesn't help much here; this is a single-boss fight.

## Fight tips

- Keep moving and watch the arena edges for incoming attacks.
- Use your strongest burst during openings rather than trying to out-damage everything.

## Rewards

Xesht can drop rare uniques that are worth a lot early in a league. Check [poe.ninja](https://poe.ninja/poe2/economy) for current values. See also our [Breach farming strategy](/farming/breach/).

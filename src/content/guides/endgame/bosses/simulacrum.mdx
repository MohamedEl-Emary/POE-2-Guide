The Simulacrum is a special [Delirium](/endgame/mechanics/delirium/) arena where you survive seven waves of increasingly dangerous monsters. Completing it is the path to the Delirium pinnacle.

## How to reach it

1. Run maps with Delirium mirrors and collect <Item name="Simulacrum Splinter" />s.
2. Combine them into a <Item name="Simulacrum" />.
3. Open it from your map device and survive all seven waves.

## Before you go in

- The waves test clear speed and defense more than boss damage.
- Bring a build that kills packs quickly. Slow builds get overwhelmed in later waves.
- Cap resistances; Delirium monsters stack effects on you.

## Tips

- Stay near the middle so monsters can't trap you against a wall.
- Use the short breaks between waves to refill flasks.
- If wave 5 or 6 kills you, practise with more Delirium in maps first.

## Rewards

The Simulacrum drops Delirium rewards and leads to the pinnacle key. See our [Delirium and boss rush strategy](/farming/delirium-boss-rush/) for how to farm it.

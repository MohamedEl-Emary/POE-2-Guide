The Arbiter of Ash is the first Atlas pinnacle boss most players meet. Beating it is a step on the way to the [Arbiter of Divinity](/endgame/bosses/arbiter-of-divinity/) and the rest of the [Atlas tree](/endgame/atlas-tree/).

## How to reach it

1. Progress through the Precursor Fortress until you find the boss areas that drop Crisis Fragments.
2. Collect all three: <Item name="Weathered Crisis Fragment" />, <Item name="Faded Crisis Fragment" /> and <Item name="Ancient Crisis Fragment" />.
3. Use them at the Burning Monolith to open the fight.

You can also buy fragments from other players if one area gives you trouble.

## Before you go in

- Cap fire, cold and lightning resistances at 75%. Fire resistance matters most for this fight.
- Bring a life flask with a quick recovery and keep your dodge roll free.
- Make sure your build has a single-target skill.

## Fight tips

- The Arbiter fills the arena with fire. Stay mobile and don't fight in burning ground.
- Large attacks have clear wind-ups. Dodge roll through or away from them, then punish.
- If you're dying in one hit, fix defenses before trying again; more damage won't help.

## Rewards

The kill progresses your Atlas and can drop valuable uniques. Check [poe.ninja](https://poe.ninja/poe2/economy) for what they're currently worth.

When you finish the campaign, the game opens up. Instead of a story, you get the <GlossaryTerm id="atlas" />: an endless map of areas to explore, with bosses and mechanics you can specialise in. This is where most players spend most of their time.

<Callout type="beginner">
  The endgame can feel overwhelming at first. You don't need to understand all of it. Run maps, follow the roadmap below, and add one new system at a time.
</Callout>

## Your endgame roadmap

### Step 1: Get comfortable with maps

Use <GlossaryTerm id="waystone">Waystones</GlossaryTerm> to open map nodes on the Atlas. Start with low tiers and work up. Your only goals here are to keep your resistances capped, upgrade gear and learn how maps work. See [The Atlas](/endgame/atlas/).

### Step 2: Complete your first Precursor Tower

<GlossaryTerm id="precursor-tower">Precursor Towers</GlossaryTerm> appear on the Atlas. Completing one lets you slot <GlossaryTerm id="precursor-tablet">tablets</GlossaryTerm> that boost nearby maps, and your first tower also reveals the Precursor Fortress.

### Step 3: Start earning Atlas passive points

The <GlossaryTerm id="atlas-passive-tree" /> changes how maps and mechanics work. Points come mainly from maps in the Precursor Fortress. Spend them on one or two mechanics you enjoy.

### Step 4: Pick a mechanic to specialise in

Each endgame mechanic plays differently and rewards different things:

| Mechanic | In one line |
|---|---|
| [Breach](/endgame/mechanics/breach/) | Hold back an expanding rift of monsters |
| [Delirium](/endgame/mechanics/delirium/) | Push into a fog that makes monsters stronger and richer |
| [Expedition](/endgame/mechanics/expedition/) | Blow up the ground to dig out treasure |
| [Ritual](/endgame/mechanics/ritual/) | Fight at altars and spend tribute on rewards |
| [Abyss](/endgame/mechanics/abyss/) | Follow cracks in the ground to Abyssal bosses |
| [Trial of Chaos](/endgame/mechanics/trial-of-chaos/) | Survive escalating challenge rooms |

### Step 5: Take on pinnacle bosses

<GlossaryTerm id="pinnacle-boss">Pinnacle bosses</GlossaryTerm> are the hardest fights in the game. They need strong single-target damage and solid defences, so treat them as a long-term goal.

### Step 6: Farm currency

Once your character is stable, the endgame becomes about making currency to fund upgrades. The [Farming hub](/farming/) explains how that works.

## Signs you're ready to push higher

- Your resistances are capped and you rarely die.
- You can clear your current map tier quickly.
- You're finding upgrades less often at your current tier.

If you're dying a lot, drop down a tier and upgrade first. There's no penalty for going slower.

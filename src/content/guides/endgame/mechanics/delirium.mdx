<GlossaryTerm id="delirium" /> starts with a strange mirror in a map. Touch it, and a fog spreads out from it.

## How it works

Inside the fog, monsters are **stronger and drop more**. The further you push from the mirror, the more dangerous and rewarding the fog becomes. After a while, the fog fades and things return to normal.

## What you get

- Extra rewards and more valuable monsters while the fog lasts.
- <Item name="Simulacrum Splinter">Simulacrum splinters</Item>, which combine into a <Item name="Simulacrum" />. It opens a wave-based survival encounter.
- Distilled emotions such as <Item name="Liquid Paranoia" />, which add a modifier to a jewel. They can also be traded.

## Tips for beginners

<Callout type="warning">
  The fog scales quickly. If enemies suddenly start hurting a lot, slow down or let the fog fade. Pushing too far is the most common way to die in Delirium.
</Callout>

- Clear fast while the fog is weak, and be cautious deeper in.
- Bring strong defences. Delirium monsters gain extra power.

## Is it a good farm?

Delirium paired with boss rushing was one of the stronger 0.5.5 farms, but it's demanding on your character. Try it once your build is comfortable in higher tiers.

<GlossaryTerm id="expedition" /> is about digging up buried treasure with explosives. It became a core mechanic in patch 0.5.5.

## How it works

1. Find the Expedition encounter in a map and talk to it to start.
2. **Place explosive charges** on the ground within a limited range.
3. **Detonate** them. The explosions reveal buried chests and monsters.
4. Defeat what you dug up and collect the loot.

## Remnants

Some buried objects are **remnants**. Each one adds a modifier that makes nearby monsters harder but improves rewards. You can see these before detonating, so you can choose to avoid the dangerous ones.

<Callout type="tip">
  Read the remnant modifiers before you place charges. If one is dangerous for your build, aim your explosives elsewhere.
</Callout>

## What you get

- Chests full of loot.
- Expedition's own currency, such as <Item name="Sun Artifact" /> and <Item name="Exotic Coinage" />, which you spend with the Expedition traders.
- <Item name="Expedition Logbook">Expedition logbooks</Item> that lead to bigger Expedition encounters.

## Is it a good farm?

Yes, especially for beginners. Expedition is forgiving because you control the difficulty, and it pays out a lot of raw currency. It was one of the most recommended starter farms in 0.5.5. See the [Farming hub](/farming/).

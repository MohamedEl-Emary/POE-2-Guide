<GlossaryTerm id="ritual" /> places altars in a map. Activate one, and monsters are summoned around it.

## How it works

1. Step into the circle of a Ritual altar to start it.
2. Fight the monsters that appear while staying **inside the circle**.
3. Complete every altar in the map to earn **tribute**.
4. Spend your tribute in the Ritual reward window on the items offered.

You can **defer** an item you can't afford yet. It may appear again in a later Ritual, so you can save up for it.

## What you get

- A selection of items to buy with tribute.
- <GlossaryTerm id="omen">Omens</GlossaryTerm>, which are valuable crafting items, are a major Ritual reward.
- Access to the Ritual pinnacle boss, **the King in the Mists**.

<Callout type="tip">
  Look out for valuable Omens in the reward window. Even if you don't use them, they often sell for a lot.
</Callout>

## Tips for beginners

- Ritual monsters are tougher than normal. Make sure you can clear comfortably first.
- Staying in the circle matters, so builds that kill quickly from where they stand do well.

## Is it a good farm?

Ritual becomes a good farm once your character clears comfortably, and the King in the Mists can be very profitable for builds with strong single-target damage.

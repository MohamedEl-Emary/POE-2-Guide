A <GlossaryTerm id="breach" /> is a hand-shaped rift that appears in a map. Touch it and it opens, pouring out monsters from another world.

## How it works

The rift **keeps expanding** as long as you keep killing the monsters inside it. If you stop killing, it shrinks and closes. The goal is to keep the momentum going for as long as you can.

## What you get

- Lots of monsters, which means lots of experience and drops.
- <Item name="Breach Splinter">Breach splinters</Item>, which combine into a <Item name="Breachstone" />, the item that opens the Breach pinnacle encounter.
- Breach-specific rewards that can be valuable to other players.

The Breach pinnacle encounter is a boss fight against **Xesht, We That Are One**, opened through these Breach items.

## Tips for beginners

<Callout type="tip">
  Breach rewards fast clearing. The quicker you kill, the bigger the rift grows. Builds with large areas of effect do best.
</Callout>

- Keep moving around the edge of the rift instead of running to the middle.
- Don't chase single monsters. Head for the dense packs.
- Breach monsters hit hard in groups. Make sure your defences are solid.

## Is it a good farm?

Breach is one of the stronger farming mechanics, but it favours characters with strong clear speed. See the [Farming hub](/farming/) for how to choose a farm.

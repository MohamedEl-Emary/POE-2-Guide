The <GlossaryTerm id="abyss" /> appears as a crack in the ground. Walk along it, and it splits open, releasing monsters.

## How it works

1. Find the start of the Abyss crack and walk along it.
2. Pits open along the path and spawn waves of monsters.
3. Keep following the crack and killing the waves until it ends.

## Abyssal Ravines

Patch 0.5.5 added **Abyssal Ravines**, longer Abyss encounters that end with a **guaranteed Abyss boss**. They're a good target once you're comfortable with normal Abyss.

## What you get

- Extra monsters and loot along the path.
- Items with **desecrated modifiers**, hidden modifiers that are revealed later, which can create unusual gear.
- Certain <GlossaryTerm id="omen">Omens</GlossaryTerm>. Abyss Omen farming was one of the strongest strategies in 0.5.5.

<Callout type="tip">
  Kill monsters as they spawn instead of running ahead. If too many pile up, the encounter gets much harder.
</Callout>

## Is it a good farm?

Yes, for characters with good clear speed. It's more demanding than Expedition but can be very rewarding.

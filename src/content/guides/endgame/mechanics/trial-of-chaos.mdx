The <GlossaryTerm id="trial-of-chaos" /> is a series of challenge rooms that get harder as you go. It has two purposes: earning **Ascendancy points** and, in the endgame, earning valuable rewards.

## How it works

You move through a sequence of rooms, each with an objective, such as surviving or defeating certain enemies. Between rooms you choose modifiers that make the trial harder. Your goal is to reach the end without failing.

## Changes in 0.5.5

Patch 0.5.5 reworked the Trial of Chaos:

- You can **pause between rooms**.
- Rewards are given **per room** rather than only at the end.
- All three **Trialmaster fragments** can be earned in a single 30-room run.

## For Ascendancy points

Together with the <GlossaryTerm id="trial-of-the-sekhemas" />, the Trial of Chaos is how you earn up to **eight Ascendancy points**. Many players prefer one trial or the other depending on their build.

<Callout type="beginner">
  Read each modifier before you accept it. Some are harmless for your build and some are deadly. Choose the ones you can handle.
</Callout>

## Is it worth doing in the endgame?

Once you have the Ascendancy points you need, the endgame version is optional, but its per-room rewards after 0.5.5 make it more attractive than before.

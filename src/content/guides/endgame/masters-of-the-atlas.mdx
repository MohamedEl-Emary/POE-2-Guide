Patch 0.5 added three **Masters of the Atlas**. Think of them as Ascendancies for the endgame: each has a small tree of powerful nodes that change how maps play. You unlock nodes by finishing each Master's missions.

## How Masters work

- Each Master has **12 nodes in rows of three**.
- Completing a Master's missions unlocks more of their nodes.
- You choose **four nodes per Master** to use.
- You can swap your picks before starting a map, so you can tailor them to what you're running.

<Callout type="warning">
  Exactly how many Masters can be active at the same time isn't spelled out clearly in the patch notes. Check in game before planning a setup around all three at once.
</Callout>

## The three Masters

| Master | Focus | Missions |
|---|---|---|
| **Hilda** | Boss hunting and beasts | Kill Great Beasts in tier 6, 11, 14 and 15 maps |
| **Doryani** | Map control, travel and terraforming | Clear Corrupted Nexuses in tier 6, 11 and 14 maps, then face the Immured Fury in tier 15 |
| **Jado** | Unique rewards | Complete four Anomaly maps from tier 6: the Jade Isles, the Sealed Vault, the Derelict Manor and the Sealed Reservoir |

### Hilda

Hilda's camp is south-west of where your Atlas starts. Her nodes improve boss encounters and beasts. Breeding Season and Mighty Prey are common early picks.

### Doryani

Doryani is about controlling the Atlas itself: moving faster across it and reshaping it. Stitch the Flesh is a popular first pick.

### Jado

Jado's Anomaly maps reward unique items, including Lineage support gems that some top builds use. If you want those supports for your build, start his missions early.

## What a beginner should do

1. Unlock Hilda first. Her missions line up with normal map progression.
2. Do Doryani's tier 6 Nexus when you reach it; early travel nodes save time.
3. Run Jado's Anomaly maps when you have a build that needs Lineage supports, or when you want to try for valuable uniques.

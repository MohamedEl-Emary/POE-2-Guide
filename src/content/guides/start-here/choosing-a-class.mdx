There's no wrong answer here. Your class sets your starting weapon and where you begin on the <GlossaryTerm id="passive-tree" />, but any class can eventually use any skill. Pick the one that sounds the most fun.

<Callout type="beginner">
  Your first character is for learning. Most players make a second one within a few weeks, once they know what they enjoy.
</Callout>

## Quick picks

- **"I want to be safe while I learn."** Play a minion [Witch](/classes/witch/). Your army fights for you.
- **"I want to shoot things from far away."** Play a [Ranger](/classes/ranger/) or a [Mercenary](/classes/mercenary/).
- **"I want to cast big spells."** Play a [Sorceress](/classes/sorceress/).
- **"I want to be a tank."** Play a [Warrior](/classes/warrior/) or a [Druid](/classes/druid/).
- **"I want to move fast and hit hard."** Play a [Monk](/classes/monk/) or a [Huntress](/classes/huntress/).
- **"I want to wait for swords."** The [Duelist](/classes/duelist/) arrives at 1.0 on December 11, 2026.

## Side-by-side comparison

| Class | Fights with | Range | Main defence | First-character friendly |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Warrior](/classes/warrior/) | Maces, shields | Melee | Armour, block | Yes |
| [Sorceress](/classes/sorceress/) | Elemental spells | Ranged | Energy shield | Yes |
| [Witch](/classes/witch/) | Minions, chaos spells | Ranged | Energy shield, minions | Very |
| [Ranger](/classes/ranger/) | Bows | Ranged | Evasion | Very |
| [Monk](/classes/monk/) | Quarterstaves | Melee | Evasion, energy shield | Some experience helps |
| [Mercenary](/classes/mercenary/) | Crossbows, grenades | Ranged | Armour, evasion | Yes |
| [Huntress](/classes/huntress/) | Spears | Melee and ranged | Evasion, parry | Some experience helps |
| [Druid](/classes/druid/) | Shapeshifting, nature spells | Both | Armour, energy shield | Some experience helps |

"Friendly" is our opinion, based on how forgiving each class is while you're still learning.

## Things that matter less than you think

- **Starting stats.** You'll find gear and passives for whatever you need.
- **Being "meta".** Almost every class can finish the campaign and the early endgame comfortably.
- **Picking the "wrong" Ascendancy later.** You choose it in Act 2, after you've already played the class for a while.

## Things that do matter

- **Range.** Ranged and minion characters are much easier to keep alive while learning boss fights.
- **Defences.** Every class needs enough life or <GlossaryTerm id="energy-shield" /> and capped <GlossaryTerm id="resistances" />. Read [Core concepts](/start-here/core-concepts/) next.

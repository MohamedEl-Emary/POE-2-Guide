Trading is how most players get the gear their build needs. You don't have to trade, since <GlossaryTerm id="ssf" /> exists for players who don't want to, but in a normal league it's the fastest way to get stronger.

## The two ways to trade

### 1. The official trade site (items)

For items like rare gear and uniques, use the official trade website (link on our [tools page](/tools/)). Make sure the league selector matches the league you play in.

A typical trade goes like this:

1. **Search** for an item by its type and the stats you want.
2. **Contact the seller** using the button on the listing. This sends them a ready-made message in game.
3. The seller **invites you to their party**. Accept, then visit their <GlossaryTerm id="hideout" />.
4. Open the **trade window**, check the item, add your payment and accept.

<Callout type="warning" title="Always check before you accept">
  Hover over the item inside the trade window and confirm it's exactly what you paid for. The trade window is the only safe way to trade.
</Callout>

### 2. The Currency Exchange (currency)

For swapping one kind of currency for another, such as many <GlossaryTerm id="exalted-orb">Exalted Orbs</GlossaryTerm> for a <GlossaryTerm id="divine-orb" />, use the in-game <GlossaryTerm id="currency-exchange" />. You set the rate you want, and the order fills when another player matches it. No messaging needed.

## How to know what things are worth

Prices change constantly, so never trust a fixed number you read somewhere, including on this site. Check:

- **poe.ninja** for live currency and unique prices.
- **The trade site** for similar rare items. Look at what items are actually listed for, not the single cheapest outlier.

## Selling your items

To sell, put the item in a public stash tab and give it a price. Buyers will contact you through the trade site. Answer when you can. If you're busy, it's fine to ignore a message.

## Basic etiquette

- Reply politely, even to say "sold".
- Don't haggle in the first message unless the listing invites offers.
- If you're no longer selling something, take it out of your public tab.
- Ignore anyone asking you to trade outside the trade window.

<Callout type="beginner">
  Your first goal in trading is simple: sell anything valuable you don't need, and spend it on resistances and life for your gear.
</Callout>

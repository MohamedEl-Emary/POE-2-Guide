Path of Exile 2 drops a lot of items, and most of them aren't worth picking up. A <GlossaryTerm id="loot-filter" /> decides what's shown on the ground, how it's labelled and what sound plays, so valuable drops stand out and junk disappears.

<Callout type="beginner">
  You don't need a filter for your very first hours, but install one before you finish the campaign. Without it, it's very easy to walk past something valuable.
</Callout>

## Why use a community filter?

The game comes with a basic filter, but community filters are far more detailed. The most popular one is maintained by a player known as **NeverSink**. It's kept up to date each patch and highlights currency, valuable bases and uniques clearly.

## How to install one

The easiest way is through **FilterBlade**, the website that hosts NeverSink's filter (link on our [tools page](/tools/)).

1. Open FilterBlade and choose Path of Exile 2.
2. Pick a **strictness** level (see below) and, optionally, a visual style.
3. Sign in with your Path of Exile account and **sync** the filter to your account. This works on PC and console.
4. In the game, open **Options → Game** and select the filter from the item filter list.

You can also download the filter file and place it in the game's filter folder on PC. Syncing is simpler, and it updates in one click.

## Choosing a strictness

Strictness controls how much the filter hides.

- **Campaign:** use a soft or regular setting. You need lots of basic items for upgrades.
- **Early maps:** move to semi-strict once you're picking up too much junk.
- **Later endgame:** strict or very strict, so you only see items worth your time.

<Callout type="tip">
  If you think a filter is hiding something you need, step down one strictness level. It's better to see a little too much than to miss upgrades.
</Callout>

## Updating your filter

Filters need updates when the game changes, especially when a new patch or league starts. If you synced through FilterBlade, re-sync after each major patch, such as the 1.0 launch.

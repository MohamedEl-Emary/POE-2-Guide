## Is Path of Exile 2 free?

Not yet. During Early Access the game is paid. At the **1.0 launch on December 11, 2026**, it becomes free-to-play. Optional purchases are cosmetics and convenience items such as extra stash tabs.

## Do I need to have played the first Path of Exile?

No. Path of Exile 2 is a separate game with its own story, and this site assumes you've never played either.

## Can I play with a controller?

Yes. Controllers are fully supported on PC, and the game is also on consoles.

## Can I play solo?

Absolutely. Most players play the campaign and endgame alone. Grouping up is optional.

## Which league should I play?

Play the newest normal **trade league**. Everyone starts from zero there, so the economy is friendly to new players. Avoid <GlossaryTerm id="hardcore" /> and <GlossaryTerm id="ssf" /> until you know the game.

## What happens when a league ends?

Characters from a finished league normally move to the permanent **Standard** league, so you don't lose them. New leagues begin with a fresh start for everyone.

## I made a mistake on my passive tree. Is my character ruined?

No. You can refund passive points for <GlossaryTerm id="gold" />. See <GlossaryTerm id="respec" />.

## Can I change my class later?

No, but you can make another character. Many players do once they know what they enjoy.

## Why do I keep dying?

The most common reasons, in order:

1. **Uncapped resistances.** Check your character sheet. See [Core concepts](/start-here/core-concepts/).
2. **Too little life or energy shield** for your level.
3. **Standing still** during a boss's big attack. Use your <GlossaryTerm id="dodge-roll" />.
4. **An outdated weapon.** Damage that's too low means fights last too long.

## How do I get Divine Orbs?

Mostly by selling valuable loot and converting it on the <GlossaryTerm id="currency-exchange" />, not by finding them directly. The farming section explains this in detail.

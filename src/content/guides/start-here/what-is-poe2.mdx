Path of Exile 2 (PoE2) is an action role-playing game made by Grinding Gear Games. You view your character from above, fight large groups of monsters, pick up loot, and slowly turn a weak level 1 character into something powerful. If you have played Diablo, the basic loop will feel familiar. The depth is what sets it apart.

## The core loop

Almost everything in the game feeds one cycle:

1. **Kill monsters** in a zone.
2. **Pick up items and currency** they drop.
3. **Make your character stronger** with better gear, new skills and passive points.
4. **Move on to harder content**, which drops better rewards.

<Callout type="beginner">
  You don't need to understand every system before you start. Play through the campaign, pick up what looks useful, and come back to these guides when something confuses you.
</Callout>

## How your character grows

There are three main sources of power, and every build guide on this site is built around them.

- **Skills** come from <GlossaryTerm id="skill-gem">skill gems</GlossaryTerm>. You customise them with <GlossaryTerm id="support-gem">support gems</GlossaryTerm>, which change how the skill behaves.
- **The <GlossaryTerm id="passive-tree" />** gives you a point to spend on small stat bonuses most times you level up.
- **Your <GlossaryTerm id="ascendancy" />** is a specialisation you unlock during the campaign. It adds a handful of very strong bonuses that define your play style.

Gear ties it all together. Items roll random modifiers, which is why loot is exciting and why trading and crafting matter.

## The two halves of the game

### The campaign

The story is played through a series of <GlossaryTerm id="act">Acts</GlossaryTerm>. In patch 0.5.5 that means Acts 1 to 4 followed by three shorter Interludes. Most new players finish it somewhere around level 60 to 65.

<Callout type="warning" title="Changing at 1.0">
  The 1.0 release on December 11, 2026 replaces the Interludes with six full Acts. Our leveling guide will be rewritten for that structure at launch.
</Callout>

### The endgame

After the campaign you unlock the <GlossaryTerm id="atlas" />, a large map of endgame areas. You open areas on it with <GlossaryTerm id="waystone">Waystones</GlossaryTerm>, fight pinnacle bosses, and specialise in the mechanics you enjoy farming. This is where most players spend the majority of their time.

## Currency instead of gold

PoE2's economy is built on **currency items**: orbs that also work as crafting tools. The <GlossaryTerm id="exalted-orb" /> is the everyday trade currency, while the <GlossaryTerm id="divine-orb" /> is used for expensive deals. Exchange rates between them move constantly, so always check a live price site rather than a number you read in a guide.

## Leagues and game modes

Content is released in <GlossaryTerm id="league">leagues</GlossaryTerm>. A new league means a fresh economy where everyone starts from zero, which makes it the best time to begin. You can also choose stricter modes such as <GlossaryTerm id="hardcore" /> or <GlossaryTerm id="ssf" />, but we recommend a normal trade league for your first character.

## What changes at 1.0

| | Now (0.5.5) | At 1.0 (Dec 11, 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Paid Early Access | Free-to-play |
| Campaign | 4 Acts + 3 Interludes | 6 Acts |
| Classes | 8 | 9, with the Duelist added |

The Duelist's Ascendancies had not been announced as of October 24, 2026.

New players often ask how to "farm Divines". The honest answer: you don't farm <Item name="Divine Orb" />s directly. You farm valuable loot, sell it, and convert what you earn on the in-game <GlossaryTerm id="currency-exchange" />. This page gives you a simple plan for doing that.

<Callout type="beginner">
  If you haven't read it yet, start with [how currency works](/farming/). It explains why Divines are rare and how the economy fits together.
</Callout>

## Step 1: make your character safe first

Farming only pays if you don't die. Before you focus on money:

- Cap your fire, cold and lightning <GlossaryTerm id="resistances" /> at 75%.
- Reach a comfortable life or energy shield total for your build.
- Clear tier 10 maps without dying.

Dying in a map loses its rewards. One safe map is worth more than two failed ones.

## Step 2: sell what you already find

Even without a strategy, maps drop sellable items:

- **Currency:** <Item name="Exalted Orb" />s, <Item name="Chaos Orb" />s, <Item name="Regal Orb" />s and others. Trade them on the Currency Exchange.
- **Waystones:** sell tiers you don't need.
- **Uniques:** check each one on the trade site. Most are cheap; a few are worth a lot.
- **Essences, Omens and fragments:** these often sell well in bulk.

Use a strict <GlossaryTerm id="loot-filter" /> so you see only items worth picking up.

## Step 3: pick one strategy

Specialising is the biggest jump in income. Choose **one** mechanic, put your Atlas points into it, and stick with it for a while.

| Strategy | Investment | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| [Expedition](/farming/expedition/) | Low | Almost every build; very forgiving |
| [Citadel fragments](/farming/citadel-fragments/) | Low | Builds with good boss damage |
| [Ritual](/farming/ritual/) | Medium | Builds that clear waves well |
| [Abyss omen farming](/farming/abyss-omens/) | Medium | Fast builds |
| [Breach](/farming/breach/) | Medium to high | Fast, high-damage builds |
| [Delirium and boss rush](/farming/delirium-boss-rush/) | High | Strong, well-geared builds |

If you can't decide, start with **Expedition**.

## Step 4: add more rewards slowly

Once your strategy runs smoothly:

1. Run higher-tier <GlossaryTerm id="waystone">Waystones</GlossaryTerm> (tiers 15 and 16).
2. Add <GlossaryTerm id="precursor-tablet">Precursor Tablets</GlossaryTerm> for your mechanic to towers.
3. Add some item rarity to your gear.
4. Add map modifiers that your build can handle.

This is called <GlossaryTerm id="juicing" />. Add one layer at a time and watch whether you start dying.

## Converting to Divine Orbs

Open the Currency Exchange in town and trade your Exalted Orbs or other currency for Divines. Exchange rates change every day, so check [poe.ninja](https://poe.ninja/poe2/currency) rather than trusting a number from a guide.

## Common mistakes

- **Chasing the best strategy before the character is ready.** Gear first.
- **Doing a bit of everything.** Specialise.
- **Spending currency on random crafts.** Save it until you have a plan. See [crafting basics](/crafting/).
- **Selling too cheap.** Check the price before you list an item.

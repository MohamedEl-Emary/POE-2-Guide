[Breach](/endgame/mechanics/breach/) opens a growing rift full of monsters. The more you kill, the bigger it grows and the more it drops. Fast characters can turn a single Breach into a lot of loot. The community calls the high-end version "Highblood" after the most valuable Breach monsters.

## Why it makes money

Breach drops <Item name="Breach Splinter" />s (which become <Item name="Breachstone" />s), catalysts such as <Item name="Flesh Catalyst" /> and <Item name="Neural Catalyst" />, <Item name="Breach Ring" />s and <Item name="Essence of the Breach" />. Splinters and catalysts sell in bulk.

## Requirements

- Breach nodes on the [Atlas tree](/endgame/atlas-tree/).
- <Item name="Breach Tablet" />s for towers.
- A fast build like [Twister Spirit Walker](/builds/twister-spirit-walker/) or [Ice Shot Deadeye](/builds/ice-shot-deadeye/).

## Setup

1. Take Breach nodes on the Atlas tree.
2. Add Breach Tablets to towers near your maps.
3. Run tier 15 or 16 Waystones.

## In-map execution

1. Open the Breach and keep killing to expand it.
2. Move along the edge so new monsters keep spawning in front of you.
3. Kill everything before the Breach closes.

## What to pick up and sell

- **Splinters:** sell in bulk or combine into Breachstones.
- **Catalysts:** sell in bulk on the <GlossaryTerm id="currency-exchange" />.
- **Rings:** check prices; good rolls sell well.

## Budget vs full version

| Budget | Full |
|---|---|
| Atlas nodes only | Nodes plus Breach Tablets everywhere |
| Normal Breaches | Push toward the most valuable Breach monsters |

## Risks

- **Slow builds.** Breach rewards depend on kill speed.
- **Getting swarmed.** Keep moving along the edge.

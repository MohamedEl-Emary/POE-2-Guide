[Ritual](/endgame/mechanics/ritual/) altars summon waves of monsters. Completing them earns tribute, which you spend on rewards at the end of the map. Many of the best rewards are <GlossaryTerm id="omen">Omens</GlossaryTerm>, which crafters always need.

## Why it makes money

Omens like <Item name="Omen of Greater Exaltation" />, <Item name="Omen of Sinistral Exaltation" /> and <Item name="Omen of Whittling" /> change how crafting currency works, and players who craft high-end items pay well for them. Ritual also leads to the [King in the Mists](/endgame/bosses/king-in-the-mists/), whose drops can be valuable.

## Requirements

- Ritual nodes on the [Atlas tree](/endgame/atlas-tree/).
- <Item name="Ritual Tablet" />s for towers once you can afford them.
- A build that handles waves; see the [Whirling Assault Martial Artist](/builds/whirling-assault-martial-artist/) or [Plant Oracle](/builds/plant-oracle/).

## Setup

1. Take Ritual nodes on the Atlas tree.
2. Place Ritual Tablets in towers near your maps.
3. Run tier 15 or 16 Waystones.

## In-map execution

1. Complete every Ritual altar in the map.
2. At the last altar, open the reward window.
3. Buy the Omens and other valuable items. You can defer items you can't afford yet to a later map.

<Callout type="tip">
  Deferring a reward lets it come back in a later Ritual, often cheaper. Use it on expensive Omens.
</Callout>

## What to pick up and sell

- **Omens:** sell them on the <GlossaryTerm id="currency-exchange" /> or through trade.
- **The King in the Mists' drops:** check prices before selling.

## Budget vs full version

| Budget | Full |
|---|---|
| Atlas nodes only | Atlas nodes plus Ritual Tablets in every tower |
| Buy what you can afford | Defer expensive rewards across maps |

## Risks

- **Getting surrounded.** Ritual waves close in fast. Clear from the edge.
- **Buying cheap rewards.** Save tribute for valuable Omens.
- **Ignoring prices.** Omen values swing; check before you sell.

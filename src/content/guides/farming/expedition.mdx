## Summary

| | |
|---|---|
| Investment | Low |
| Build requirements | Any build that clears tier 15 maps safely |
| Expected profit | Steady; low to medium per map. Check [poe.ninja](https://poe.ninja/poe2/economy) for current values |

[Expedition](/endgame/mechanics/expedition/) has you place explosives to dig up buried chests and monsters. You choose how much danger to add, which makes it the most forgiving farm in 0.5.5. Patch 0.5.5 made Expedition a core mechanic, so it's available in every league.

## Why it makes money

Expedition pays in currency and in artifacts such as <Item name="Black Scythe Artifact" />, <Item name="Broken Circle Artifact" />, <Item name="Order Artifact" /> and <Item name="Exotic Coinage" />, which you trade with the Expedition vendors for items and currency. <Item name="Expedition Logbook" />s add whole Expedition areas with even more chests. Because the rewards are mostly currency, you don't need to know item prices well.

## Requirements

- Atlas points in the Expedition section of the [Atlas tree](/endgame/atlas-tree/).
- Expedition <GlossaryTerm id="precursor-tablet">tablets</GlossaryTerm> once you can afford them (optional at first).
- A build that clears tier 15 maps without dying.

## Setup

1. Take Expedition nodes on the Atlas tree, starting with ones that make Expedition more common.
2. Run tier 15 or 16 Waystones.
3. When you can, add Expedition tablets to towers near the maps you plan to run.

## In-map execution

1. Find the Expedition encounter and place your explosives.
2. Chain explosives to cover as many chests and remnants as possible.
3. Read the remnant modifiers before you detonate. Skip any your build can't handle (for example, reflected damage or no regeneration).
4. Detonate, kill the monsters, and open the chests.

<Callout type="tip">
  Remnants with reward modifiers also make monsters harder. Start with fewer, then add more as you get comfortable.
</Callout>

## What to pick up and sell

- **Currency:** take it all; trade it on the <GlossaryTerm id="currency-exchange" />.
- **Artifacts and coinage:** spend them at the Expedition vendors, or trade them.
- **Logbooks:** run them or sell them.

## Budget vs full version

| Budget | Full |
|---|---|
| Atlas nodes only | Atlas nodes plus Expedition tablets in every tower |
| Tier 15 maps | Tier 16 maps with extra modifiers and item rarity |
| Avoid dangerous remnants | Take more remnants for bigger rewards |

## Risks

- **Bad remnant choices.** Always read them; one bad modifier can end a run.
- **Under-geared builds.** Expedition monsters hit hard with many remnants.
- **Hoarding artifacts.** Spend or sell them regularly; they're income.

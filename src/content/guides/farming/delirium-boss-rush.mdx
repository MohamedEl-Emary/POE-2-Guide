## Summary

| | |
|---|---|
| Investment | High |
| Build requirements | Strong clear, strong single target and solid defenses |
| Expected profit | High at the top end; risky for under-geared builds. Check [poe.ninja](https://poe.ninja/poe2/economy) |

This strategy combines [Delirium](/endgame/mechanics/delirium/) in maps with running bosses. Delirium fog makes monsters stronger and more rewarding, then you use the fragments and splinters you collect to run [the Simulacrum](/endgame/bosses/simulacrum/) and other pinnacle bosses.

## Why it makes money

Delirium drops <Item name="Simulacrum Splinter" />s, Liquid Emotions such as <Item name="Diluted Liquid Ire" /> and <Item name="Liquid Paranoia" /> (used to anoint), and <Item name="Essence of Delirium" />. Bosses drop valuable uniques.

## Requirements

- Delirium nodes on the [Atlas tree](/endgame/atlas-tree/).
- <Item name="Delirium Tablet" />s.
- A well-geared build such as [Flameblast Oil Grenade Gemling](/builds/flameblast-oil-grenade-gemling/).

## Setup

1. Take Delirium and boss nodes on the Atlas tree.
2. Add Delirium Tablets to towers.
3. Run tier 16 Waystones.

## In-map execution

1. Enter the Delirium mirror and clear quickly while the fog spreads.
2. Collect splinters and emotions.
3. Combine splinters into a <Item name="Simulacrum" /> and run it, or sell the splinters.

## What to pick up and sell

- **Splinters and Simulacrums:** sell or run them.
- **Liquid Emotions:** sell in bulk.
- **Boss uniques:** check prices before selling.

## Budget vs full version

| Budget | Full |
|---|---|
| Sell splinters | Run Simulacrums and pinnacle bosses yourself |
| A few tablets | Delirium Tablets in every tower |

## Risks

- **Deaths are expensive.** Fog and bosses punish weak builds.
- **Over-investing too early.** Build up from Expedition or Ritual first.

## Summary

| | |
|---|---|
| Investment | Low |
| Build requirements | Good single-target damage and solid defenses |
| Expected profit | Varies with demand; usually higher early in a league. Check [poe.ninja](https://poe.ninja/poe2/economy) |

The Crisis Fragments that open the [Arbiter of Ash](/endgame/bosses/arbiter-of-ash/) come from boss areas in the Atlas. Many players want to fight the Arbiter but don't want to hunt fragments, so a steady supply sells well.

## Why it makes money

<Item name="Weathered Crisis Fragment" />, <Item name="Faded Crisis Fragment" /> and <Item name="Ancient Crisis Fragment" /> are needed by everyone progressing their Atlas. Demand is highest in the first weeks of a league, when most players are pushing their Atlas.

## Requirements

- A build that kills bosses quickly and safely (see builds like [Flameblast Oil Grenade Gemling](/builds/flameblast-oil-grenade-gemling/)).
- Atlas nodes that improve boss encounters. Hilda's nodes from [Masters of the Atlas](/endgame/masters-of-the-atlas/) help here.

## Setup

1. Progress your Atlas until you can find the fragment boss areas reliably.
2. Take boss-focused Atlas nodes and Hilda's boss nodes.
3. Keep plenty of Waystones so you can travel across the Atlas.

## In-map execution

1. Head straight for the boss. Don't full-clear the map.
2. Kill the boss and grab the fragment.
3. Move on to the next fragment area.

## What to pick up and sell

- **Crisis Fragments:** sell in full sets of three when possible; sets sell faster.
- **Boss drops:** check uniques on the trade site.

## Budget vs full version

| Budget | Full |
|---|---|
| Sell fragments | Run the Arbiter yourself and sell the boss drops |
| Atlas nodes only | Atlas nodes plus Hilda's boss nodes |

## Risks

- **Falling prices.** Demand drops later in the league. Check prices before committing.
- **Weak single target.** Slow kills make this strategy unprofitable.

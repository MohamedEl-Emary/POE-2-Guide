[Abyss](/endgame/mechanics/abyss/) cracks open across the map and spawns monsters from pits. Patch 0.5.5 added Abyssal Ravines with a guaranteed Abyss boss at the end. The strategy is to clear as many pits as possible and sell the rewards.

## Why it makes money

Abyss drops bones such as <Item name="Gnawed Jawbone" />, <Item name="Gnawed Rib" /> and <Item name="Gnawed Collarbone" /> (with stronger Ancient versions), which crafters use to add special modifiers. It also drops Omens such as <Item name="Omen of Abyssal Echoes" />, and <Item name="Essence of the Abyss" />.

## Requirements

- Abyss nodes on the [Atlas tree](/endgame/atlas-tree/).
- <Item name="Abyss Tablet" />s for towers.
- A fast build, such as [Galvanic Shards Tactician](/builds/galvanic-shards-tactician/) or [Poisonburst Arrow Pathfinder](/builds/poisonburst-arrow-pathfinder/).

## Setup

1. Take Abyss nodes on the Atlas tree.
2. Add Abyss Tablets to towers near your maps.
3. Run tier 15 or 16 Waystones.

## In-map execution

1. Follow the Abyss crack and kill monsters as pits open.
2. Clear pits quickly; they keep spawning while open.
3. If a Ravine appears, go down and kill the boss at the end.

## What to pick up and sell

- **Bones:** sell in bulk; Ancient versions are worth more.
- **Omens and essences:** sell on the <GlossaryTerm id="currency-exchange" /> or through trade.

## Budget vs full version

| Budget | Full |
|---|---|
| Atlas nodes only | Nodes plus Abyss Tablets in every tower |
| Skip Ravines you can't handle | Run every Ravine and boss |

## Risks

- **Slow clear.** Pits overwhelm slow builds.
- **Ravine bosses.** Make sure your single target can handle them.

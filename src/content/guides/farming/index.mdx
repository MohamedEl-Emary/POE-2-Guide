Every new player asks the same question: **"How do I farm Divine Orbs?"** The honest answer is that you mostly don't. You farm *value*, and then you convert it.

## The one idea to remember

<GlossaryTerm id="divine-orb">Divine Orbs</GlossaryTerm> drop rarely, and there's no vendor recipe for them. Players who seem to earn lots of them are doing this:

1. **Farm** content that drops valuable loot.
2. **Sell** that loot to other players, usually for <GlossaryTerm id="exalted-orb">Exalted Orbs</GlossaryTerm>.
3. **Convert** the Exalted Orbs into Divine Orbs on the <GlossaryTerm id="currency-exchange" />.

<Callout type="beginner">
  Think of Divine Orbs as a savings account. You fill it by selling things, not by waiting for one to drop.
</Callout>

## Why prices aren't on this site

Exchange rates and item prices change every day, and they swing a lot at the start of each league. We never quote fixed numbers. Instead, check **poe.ninja** for live prices (see [Tools](/tools/)).

## What makes something valuable?

Items are valuable when lots of players need them and few drop. Common examples:

- Crafting currency like Greater and Perfect orbs, <GlossaryTerm id="omen">Omens</GlossaryTerm> and high-tier <GlossaryTerm id="essence">Essences</GlossaryTerm>.
- Items that open special encounters, such as boss fragments and splinters.
- Rare items with strong modifiers that many builds want.
- Popular <GlossaryTerm id="unique-item">unique items</GlossaryTerm>.

## How rewards scale

Your income depends on four things:

| Factor | What it means |
|---|---|
| Map tier | Higher-tier <GlossaryTerm id="waystone">Waystones</GlossaryTerm> drop better loot |
| Map modifiers and tablets | More danger, more rewards. See <GlossaryTerm id="juicing" /> |
| Item rarity | More rarity on your gear improves the quality of drops |
| Specialisation | Focusing your Atlas on **one** mechanic multiplies its rewards |

## Choosing your first farm

Pick one mechanic and commit to it, rather than doing a bit of everything.

- **Low investment, forgiving:** [Expedition](/endgame/mechanics/expedition/). You control the difficulty, and it pays in raw currency.
- **Once you clear well:** [Ritual](/endgame/mechanics/ritual/) for Omens.
- **Fast characters:** [Breach](/endgame/mechanics/breach/) and [Abyss](/endgame/mechanics/abyss/).
- **Strong bosses:** boss fights like the King in the Mists, if your single-target damage is high.

Start with our [beginner farming guide](/farming/beginner-farming/), then pick a strategy: [Expedition](/farming/expedition/), [Citadel fragments](/farming/citadel-fragments/), [Ritual](/farming/ritual/), [Abyss](/farming/abyss-omens/), [Breach](/farming/breach/) or [Delirium and boss rush](/farming/delirium-boss-rush/).

## Common mistakes

- Spending Exalted Orbs on random crafts instead of saving or trading them.
- Running maps that are too hard, dying and losing progress.
- Picking up everything instead of using a strict <GlossaryTerm id="loot-filter" />.
- Trusting a price you read in an old guide.

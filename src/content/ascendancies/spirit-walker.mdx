## How it plays

The Spirit Walker surrounds you with spirit animals. Moving, attacking and dodging all feed resources that unleash extra skills.

## Who it's for

Twister Spirit Walker was one of the fastest mapping builds in 0.5.5. It asks you to track a few resources, but the reward is a very fast character.

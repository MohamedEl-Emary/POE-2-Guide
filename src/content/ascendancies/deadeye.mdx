## How it plays

The Deadeye is about raw ranged power. More arrows mean more damage and faster clearing, and Tailwind keeps you quick as long as you avoid getting hit.

## Who it's for

This is the most beginner-friendly Ranger choice and one of the safest Ascendancies in the game. <Item name="Ice Shot" /> Deadeye was a reliable 0.5.5 starter.

## How it plays

The Warbringer leans into the Warrior's loud, aggressive side. You plant totems that fight alongside you, shout war cries to empower your hits, and crack enemy armour so everything you do lands harder.

## Who it's for

Pick the Warbringer if you like mixing your own attacks with helpers like totems, or if you want a shield-based Warrior that blocks a lot of incoming damage.

<Callout type="beginner">
  War cries are a great habit to learn on any Warrior. Shout before a big slam and the difference is easy to feel.
</Callout>

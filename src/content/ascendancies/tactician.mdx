## How it plays

The Tactician controls fights. Pins hold enemies in place, banners keep you strong and supporting fire adds damage you don't have to aim yourself.

## Who it's for

This suits players who like control and support effects. <Item name="Galvanic Shards" /> Tactician was among the popular 0.5.5 builds.

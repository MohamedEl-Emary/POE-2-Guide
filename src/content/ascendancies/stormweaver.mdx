## How it plays

The Stormweaver is the most direct way to make elemental spells stronger. Storms add extra damage on top of your normal casting, and shock stacking turns lightning into a damage amplifier for the rest of your build.

## Who it's for

Choose the Stormweaver if you want a classic mage that clears screens. It works with most elemental skills, and <Item name="Spark" /> was among the popular 0.5.5 builds.

## How it plays

The Witchhunter turns the Mercenary into a specialist against magic. Sorcery Ward absorbs elemental hits, which helps against many dangerous bosses.

## Who it's for

This is a solid defensive pick for crossbow builds, and a good fit if spellcasting enemies keep killing you.

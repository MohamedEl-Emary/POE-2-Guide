## How it plays

The Smith of Kitava treats your gear as part of your build. Your weapon becomes a fire-powered tool, a duplicate weapon joins the fight, and your chest armour can be shaped into exactly the defences you're missing.

## Who it's for

This Ascendancy suits players who enjoy planning gear and like fire-themed melee. It asks a little more thought than the Titan, but its armour options can patch holes in your defences that would otherwise cost a lot of currency.

## How it plays

The Oracle is about creative build options. Its visions add extra damage, and its passive tree access opens combinations no other Ascendancy can reach.

## Who it's for

Oracle plant builds were cheap, low-effort and tanky in 0.5.5, making the Oracle surprisingly friendly for new players despite its unusual design.

## How it plays

The Blood Mage trades safety for power. Every spell costs life, so you rely on leech and remnants to keep topped up while you fight.

## Who it's for

This is a high-skill choice. It's strong in experienced hands but punishing when you get hit at low life. New players are usually happier with the Infernalist or Lich.

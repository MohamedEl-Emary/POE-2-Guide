## How it plays

The Chronomancer's power comes from active skills that manipulate time. Used well, they let you escape deadly situations and chain your strongest spells back to back.

## Who it's for

This is a technical Ascendancy. It rewards players who already know their build and like precise timing. For a first character, the Stormweaver or Disciple of Varashta will be more comfortable.

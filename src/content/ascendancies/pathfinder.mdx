## How it plays

The Pathfinder mixes ranged attacks with alchemy. Your flasks become weapons, poison spreads through packs, and you stay mobile while doing it.

## Who it's for

Pick the Pathfinder if you enjoy managing resources and damage over time. Poisonburst Arrow was a popular Pathfinder build in 0.5.5.

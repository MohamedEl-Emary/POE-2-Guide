## How it plays

The Disciple of Varashta turns the Sorceress into a commander. Your djinn handle much of the fighting while you support them and stay out of harm's way.

## Who it's for

Pick this if you like minions but want to play a Sorceress. Djinn builds were known as safe and tanky in 0.5.5, which makes them friendly for newer players.

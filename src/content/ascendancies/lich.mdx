## How it plays

The Lich makes chaos damage and curses the centre of your build. Explosions chain through packs while your energy shield keeps you safe.

## Who it's for

The Lich suits players who like damage over time and dark-themed casters. Essence Drain with Contagion was a popular budget Lich starter in 0.5.5.

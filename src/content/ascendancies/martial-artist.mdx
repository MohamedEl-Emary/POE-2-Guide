## How it plays

The Martial Artist rewards staying in the fight. Hits build Combo, Combo powers your best skills, and Mountain's Teachings make you tougher the longer you keep attacking.

## Who it's for

This is the most comfortable Monk Ascendancy for melee players. Whirling Assault Martial Artist was a top melee starter in 0.5.5.

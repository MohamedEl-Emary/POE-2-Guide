## How it plays

The Titan does one thing very well: hit enormously hard and shrug off damage. Slams ripple out with aftershocks, stuns come easily and your body armour does more work than on any other Warrior.

## Who it's for

The Titan is the simplest Warrior Ascendancy to understand, which makes it a strong choice for a first melee character. If you want to feel unkillable while learning bosses, start here.

## How it plays

The Gemling Legionnaire doesn't change how you play so much as make every skill stronger. More supports and better gems help almost any build.

## Who it's for

This is a very flexible and beginner-friendly choice. Grenade and <Item name="Flameblast" /> <Item name="Oil Grenade" /> builds were popular Gemling options in 0.5.5.

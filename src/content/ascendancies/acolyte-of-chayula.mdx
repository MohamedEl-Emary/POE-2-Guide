## How it plays

The Acolyte is one of the strangest Ascendancies in the game. Its power comes from rewriting how your resources and defences work rather than adding raw damage.

## Who it's for

This is for experienced players who enjoy unusual builds. Poisonburst Arrow variants have used the Acolyte, but it's not a typical first choice.

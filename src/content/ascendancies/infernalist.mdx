## How it plays

The Infernalist is a flexible Ascendancy. Its hellhound helps any build, and its fire and demon-form mechanics push both spellcasters and minion users further.

## Who it's for

It's a good default for a first Witch, because its benefits work with many different skills. Expect to learn how to manage the demon form's risk as you progress.
